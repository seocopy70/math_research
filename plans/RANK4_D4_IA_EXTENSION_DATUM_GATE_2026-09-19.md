The key question is whether the degree-3 defect data can be organized canonically as one of:

### A. Quotient object
A quotient of the lift fibre/defect space by a canonically defined IA gauge subgroup.

### B. Orbit/torsor object
An IA-orbit or torsor of degree-3 defects, retaining the information lost by passing to the linear map alone.

### C. Extension/cohomological object
An extension class or equivalent filtered datum whose definition does not require choosing a preferred lift.
