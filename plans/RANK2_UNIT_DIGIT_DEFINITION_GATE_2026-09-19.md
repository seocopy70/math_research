# PLAN — Rank-2 unit-digit definition gate — 2026-09-19

## Status
**OPEN — DEFINITION / HAND-DERIVATION GATE ONLY**

This gate follows the closed Delta_q coboundary track and the existing rank-2 D4 lifting control. No new finite scan is authorized until the object below is defined independently of q and of a preferred lift.

## 0. Purpose

Test whether the first non-mod-3 digit of the relator unit
\[
u(\phi)\in \mathbf Z_3^\times
\]
contains lift-dependent information that is invisible in the induced
\[
g\in GL_2(\mathbf F_3),
\]
and whether that information is potentially q-sensitive.

The intended finite datum is
\[
\kappa(\phi):=\frac{u(\phi)-u(\phi)\bmod 3}{3}\pmod 3,
\]
equivalently the class of u(phi) in
\[
(1+3\mathbf Z_3)/(1+9\mathbf Z_3)\cong\mathbf F_3,
\]
after the mod-3 component has been separated.

**Important:** this notation is provisional until the exact decomposition and normalization of u are fixed.

## 1. Frozen rank-2 model

\[
G_q^{(2)}=\langle x_1,x_2\mid r_q=x_1^q[x_1,x_2]\rangle,
\qquad q\in\{3,\infty\}.
\]

The existing rank-2 D4 control is retained as implementation evidence only:
- D4 is the first Zassenhaus level seeing x_1^3;
- q=3 and q=infinity were tested with exact F3 Magnus arithmetic;
- the control passed on four selected representatives.

This gate does not reinterpret that result as a theorem about the full automorphism image.

## 2. D1 — exact definition of the relator unit

Let F be the free pro-3 group on x_1,x_2 and R the closed normal subgroup generated by r_q.

For an actual automorphism phi in Aut(G_q), choose a lift \tilde phi in Aut(F) preserving R.

Define u(phi) only if the following is proved:
\[
\tilde\phi(r_q)\equiv r_q^{u(\phi)}
\pmod{[R,F]},
\]
with u(phi) in Z_3^times, and the resulting scalar is independent of the chosen free lift.

The relation module convention
\[
R/[R,F]\cong\mathbf Z_3
\]
and the precise generator/sign convention must be stated explicitly.

No arbitrary free-group automorphism that fails to preserve the relator normal closure is admissible.

## 3. D2 — lift class

The object is defined on **actual Aut(G_q)-lifts**, not arbitrary lifts of a matrix in GL_2(F3).

Separate:
1. a free-group automorphism lifting a linear matrix;
2. an automorphism of G_q;
3. the induced matrix on F/Phi(F).

Only (2) is an allowed source for u(phi).

The kernel
\[
K_q=\ker(\operatorname{Aut}(G_q)\to GL_2(\mathbf F_3))
\]
is the primary test locus for genuine lift dependence.

## 4. D3 — multiplicativity / crossed law

Before calling kappa a homomorphism, prove the composition law for u.

Required derivation:
\[
u(\phi\psi)=u(\phi)u(\psi)
\]
or, if the relation module carries a nontrivial action under the chosen convention, state and prove the exact crossed law.

Only after this is established may the induced mod-9 digit be called a homomorphism.

## 5. D4 — filtration observability

Determine the smallest frozen filtration level n such that
\[
u(\phi)\bmod 9
\]
is determined by the allowed finite filtered data.

Do not assume n=4. The existing D4 control only establishes that degree-3 restricted-power data is visible there; it does not prove that the relator unit mod 9 is determined by the same quotient.

Allowed inputs must be stated exactly, e.g.
\[
(F/D_n,\;R\text{-image},\;\phi\bmod D_n)
\]
or an explicitly weaker object.

Forbidden:
- q as an input label;
- abelianization torsion order as a shortcut;
- u itself as an input;
- a preferred Nielsen lift.

## 6. D5 — relation to the mod-3 linear data

Audit the reduction
\[
u(\phi)\bmod 3
\]
against the induced linear action.

The expected relation to determinant/multiplier is a **hypothesis requiring proof**, not a frozen fact.

For q=3, note that the existing rank-2 control already shows that not every determinant-1 matrix is induced by an admissible automorphism: -I is a counterexample among the tested representatives.

Therefore:
\[
u\bmod3=\det(g)
\]
may hold on the actual automorphism image while the image itself is a proper subgroup.

This distinction must remain explicit.

## 7. D6 — q=3 versus q=infinity hand derivation

### q=infinity

The presentation becomes
\[
G_\infty^{(2)}\cong \mathbf Z_3^2.
\]

Hence for an integral lift A in GL_2(Z_3), the relator-unit candidate is expected to agree with det(A), subject to the exact relation-module convention.

This is a useful control, not yet a proof of the finite filtered observable.

### q=3

Starting from
\[
r_3=x_1^3[x_1,x_2],
\]
write the induced action of an actual automorphism on the relevant abelianized/degree-one coordinates and derive the constraint imposed by preservation of the relator.

The derivation must determine, modulo 9:
1. the possible linear matrices;
2. the corresponding u(phi);
3. whether kappa(phi) varies inside K_3;
4. whether the relation between u mod 9 and the linear matrix differs from the q=infinity case.

No assumption is made in advance about the answer.

## 8. H-1 / literature boundary

The following are external mathematical claims and are **not yet frozen**:
- the canonical Demuškin orientation can be characterized through the relator module;
- the relator scalar u agrees with the orientation character in the required convention;
- u mod 3 agrees with the GSp multiplier/determinant.

Current literature confirms that Demuškin groups possess a canonical orientation induced by the dualizing module, and standard presentations encode the invariant q, but the exact automorphism-to-relator-unit identification needed here must be sourced or derived.

Therefore these claims are tagged **EXTERNAL / TO VERIFY**, not assumed.

## 9. D7 — q-blindness requirement

The candidate kappa must be defined before comparing q=3 and q=infinity.

A definition that is literally
\[
u_3-u_\infty
\]
or otherwise inserts q into its definition fails immediately.

The q comparison occurs only after the same construction has been independently defined for both presentations.

## 10. D8 — non-coboundary / non-g-only test

The candidate fails if
\[
\kappa(\phi)=f(g)
\]
for a function of the induced finite linear matrix alone.

A stronger desired witness is
\[
\phi_1,\phi_2\in K_q,
\qquad
\kappa(\phi_1)\ne\kappa(\phi_2).
\]

If no such pair exists, the lift-digit route is closed at this level.

## 11. Decision rules

### PASS
All of D1–D8 are defined and justified, and there exists a genuine lift-dependent digit not determined by g alone. Then a small rank-2 computation may be registered.

### FAIL
The digit is determined by g alone, is not observable from the allowed filtered data, or the q=3 and q=infinity relations are identical in the relevant sense.

Then this route is closed without a rank-4 scan.

### INCONCLUSIVE
The relator-unit normalization, filtration observability, or lift-independence is not yet well-defined.

Then repair the definition; no computation.

## 12. Explicit non-goals

- No claim that the digit reconstructs the full orientation character.
- No claim that a mod-9 digit is canonical under arbitrary presentation changes.
- No return to preferred-lift/Nielsen selection.
- No use of the failed Delta_q coboundary.
- No rank-4 scan.
- No inference from q=infinity determinant behavior to q=3.

## 14. Closure result — 2026-09-19

**DECISION: FAIL / CLOSED.**

The natural rank-one quotient Q=(R/[R,F])/H2(G3,Z3) ≅ ker(F_ab -> G3_ab)=3 Z3 e1 fails lift-independence. For r=r3, the free pro-3 automorphism alpha_r defined by x1 -> x1 r and x2 -> x2 induces id on G3 but sends e1 -> 4e1, hence 3e1 -> 4(3e1). Therefore two stabilizing lifts of the same group automorphism differ by the unit 4 modulo 9 on the natural quotient.

This is a definition-level FAIL, not a computational failure. D1 fails; later gates are not reached. No CI, mod-9, IA, K3, or q-comparison scan is authorized. The route is closed unless a genuinely different object is proposed that does not use this relator-module scalar construction.
