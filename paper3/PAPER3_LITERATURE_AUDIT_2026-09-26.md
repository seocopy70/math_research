## Primary prior art checked

1. Efrat–Quadrelli, *The Kummerian Property and Maximal Pro-p Galois Groups*, Proposition 7.5: for cyclotomic pro-p pairs, the free product is Kummerian iff both factors are Kummerian.
2. Quadrelli–Weigel, *Oriented pro-ell groups with the Bogomolov–Positselski property*, Proposition 5.5: free products of Kummerian oriented pro-ell groups are Kummerian.
3. Quadrelli 2024, *Chasing Maximal Pro-p Galois Groups via 1-Cyclotomicity*: Kummerian/1-cyclotomicity criteria and Demushkin orientation background.
4. Labute 1967: canonical Demushkin orientation is classical.
5. The Paper 2 sharp affine window n_aff(k)=p^{k-1}+1 is internal preceding work, not a new ingredient of Paper 3.

## Audit outcome

The following parts of Paper 3 are not independent novelty sources:

- Kummerianity of free products: explicitly known.
- Blockwise Kummer recognition from known factor orientations: follows from known free-product Kummerianity plus the Paper 1 selector.
- Sharp affine depth: imported from Paper 2.
- No-extra-mixed-depth statement: follows from the reflector/functoriality argument once Paper 2's target calculation is available.
- f>=k power-term collapse: is the same finite-depth information-loss mechanism already isolated in Paper 2, now applied blockwise.

The remaining application-level synthesis is useful and mathematically clean, but the audited literature and the preceding papers leave no clearly independent theorem in the current manuscript.
