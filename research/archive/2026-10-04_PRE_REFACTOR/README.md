# Mathematical Research Archive

This repository preserves the research record for the ongoing study of rank-4 pro-3 Demuškin groups, Zassenhaus filtrations, graded Lie structures, degree-4 obstructions, symplectic representation theory, and the possible reconstruction of canonical orientation.

## Research status

The repository deliberately distinguishes four categories:

- **EXTERNAL** — established by published literature and used as background.
- **COMPUTED** — independently derived/calculated in this research.
- **EXTERNAL + COMPUTED** — a literature theorem combined with a calculation made here.
- **OPEN** — conjectural, incomplete, or requiring further verification.

## Current research chain

Demuškin group
→ Zassenhaus filtration
→ quadratic initial relation
→ degree-4 Lie probe
→ 45-dimensional symplectic module
→ investigation of its representation structure
→ question of whether filtered data can recover the canonical 3-adic orientation.

## Important methodological note

The mod-3 graded object and the canonical orientation valued in Z_3^× must not be conflated. In particular, failure to find a trivial representation in the degree-4 mod-3 probe does not by itself disprove a relationship with orientation.
