# E1 COVER-CHANGE AUDIT — 2026-09-19

## Goal

Test whether changing a minimal free pro-3 cover destroys the degree-(2,3) relation-jet information needed by
\[
\Theta_{R,P}(\lambda)(f)=f(P)+(\lambda\wedge f)(R).
\]

No finite computation.

## 1. Reduction between two minimal covers

Let
\[
1\to R\to F\xrightarrow{\pi}G\to1,
\qquad
1\to R'\to F'\xrightarrow{\pi'}G\to1
\]
be minimal free pro-3 presentations with rank 4.

Because both free groups have the minimal number of generators of G, a choice of lifts of a basis of
\(G/\Phi(G)\) gives a free isomorphism
\(\alpha:F\to F'\) inducing the identity on G. Thus the comparison problem reduces to the effect of a free automorphism/lift lying over \(\mathrm{id}_G\).

This is the correct reduction; it does not yet prove invariance.

## 2. The kernel is genuinely nontrivial

The earlier explicit stabilizing lift
\[
\alpha_r(x_1)=x_1r,\qquad \alpha_r(x_i)=x_i\ (i>1)
\]
with \(r\in R\) shows that a cover-change/lift over \(\mathrm{id}_G\) need not act trivially on the relation module.

For the q=3 relator, taking \(r\) to be the defining relator gives multiplication by
\(4\pmod 9\)
on the relation-module generator in the abelianized relation line.

Therefore the stronger statement
\[
R/[F,R]\text{ is canonically identified under every cover change}
\]
is FALSE.

This is important: E1 cannot be upgraded by simply declaring the full relation module intrinsic.

## 3. What survives at the level actually needed

The failure above is scalar, and the projective jet was deliberately designed to forget common scaling. There is also the relator-conjugation gauge
\[
P\mapsto P+[v,R],
\]
which is invisible after applying \(f\in V^*\).

Thus the relevant question is narrower:

> Does every change of minimal free cover act on the degree-(2,3) relation jet only by common scaling plus the gauge \(P\mapsto P+[v,R]\), at least modulo the terms invisible to \(\Theta\)?

This is not yet proved.

## 4. Conditional algebraic lemma

For a one-relator Demuškin presentation, suppose the following standard structural facts are supplied:

1. \(R/[F,R]\) is cyclic, generated by the defining relator class.
2. A cover change over \(\mathrm{id}_G\) sends that generator to \(u\) times itself for a unit \(u\in\mathbf Z_3^\times\).
3. After passing to the degree-(2,3) filtered quotient, the residual difference between the transported jet and the jet of the target presentation is generated by relator conjugation, hence has degree-3 change in \([V,R]\).

Then
\[
(R,P)\mapsto(uR,uP+[v,R])
\]
for some \(u\in\mathbf Z_3^\times\), and therefore
\[
\Theta\mapsto u\Theta.
\]
The zero set of \(\Theta\) is unchanged.

This would establish precisely the invariance needed for recovery, without falsely claiming that the relation-module generator itself is canonical.

## 5. Current decision

The explicit lift-kernel witness proves that **full relation-module cover-independence is FAIL**.

The weaker **projective-jet cover-independence** remains OPEN because the required residual-gauge lemma has not been proved.

Hence:

- Fixed-cover relation module: PASS.
- Full canonical relation module across covers: FAIL.
- Projective jet invariance under covers: OPEN.
- Recovery functional invariance under common scaling/conjugation gauge: PASS.
- E1 full intrinsicness: OPEN.

## 6. Next exact proof target

Prove or disprove the residual-gauge lemma in the degree-(2,3) quotient:

\[
\text{kernel of }\operatorname{Aut}(F)\to\operatorname{Aut}(G)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(R,P)\mapsto(uR,uP+[v,R]).
\]

If true, the projective jet becomes a viable intrinsic carrier. If false, identify the extra gauge and test whether \(\Theta\) still annihilates it. If an extra gauge survives \(\Theta\), the projective-jet route must be redesigned.
