## Correction status

**The previous 9-dimensional claim was caused by incomplete H-orbit closure in the O2-7 implementation and is deprecated.**

O2-8 used a complete closure loop over every accumulated basis vector and all five generators. Its control run gave dimension 10 for the q=3 shadow. Inspection of the two implementations identified the O2-7 defect: it propagated only the most recently appended orbit vector rather than closing under every accumulated basis vector.

Therefore the previous claims \(\dim\langle HN(d)\rangle=9\) and a resulting 1-dimensional quotient are invalid.

## Corrected mathematical result

Let \(d=[X_1^{[3]},X_2]\). The certified facts are

\[
\dim\langle H d\rangle=45=\dim W,
\]

and, with complete H-orbit closure,

\[
\boxed{\dim\langle HN(d)\rangle=10.}
\]

Since \(\langle HN(d)\rangle\subseteq U=\operatorname{im}N\) and \(\dim U=10\),

\[
\boxed{\langle HN(d)\rangle=U.}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{d\in W,\quad \langle Hd\rangle=W,\quad N(d)\in U,\quad \langle HN(d)\rangle=U.}
\]

## Consequence for O2-8

O2-8 is no longer a test of whether a 9D shadow is special. Its useful role is an audit of complete H-orbit closure. The target is now \(\dim\langle HN(d)\rangle=10\), hence equality with U.

## Boundary
