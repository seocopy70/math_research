## 1. Purpose

Determine whether the 25-dimensional middle quotient

\[
M=\ker(N)/\operatorname{im}(N)
\]

of the 45-dimensional module \(W\) has a proper nonzero \(Sp_4(\mathbb F_3)\)-invariant subspace.

The computation constructs the associative algebra generated by the five exact \(25\times25\) generator matrices on \(M\):

\[
\mathcal A=\mathbb F_3[A_1,\ldots,A_5]\subseteq M_{25}(\mathbb F_3).
\]

If \(\dim\mathcal A=25^2=625\), then \(\mathcal A=M_{25}(\mathbb F_3)\), which implies that \(M\) has no nonzero proper invariant subspace and hence is absolutely irreducible.

## 3. Structural consequence

Together with the earlier exact results, we now have

\[
0\subset U\subset K\subset W,
\]

where

\[
U=\operatorname{im}N,\qquad K=\ker N,
\]

with dimensions

\[
0<10<35<45.
\]

Moreover,

\[
U\text{ is irreducible},
\]

\[
K/U=M\text{ is absolutely irreducible},
\]

and, because \(N^2=0\) and \(\operatorname{rank}N=10\),

\[
W/K\cong U.
\]

Thus the confirmed module structure has dimensions

\[
\boxed{10\;–\;25\;–\;10}.
\]

This does **not yet** identify these modules by highest weights. In particular, the hypotheses
\[
U\cong L(2,0),\qquad M\cong L(2,1),\qquad W\cong T(2,1)
\]
remain to be independently verified against the modular representation-theory literature/data.

## 4. Interpretation and logical boundary

This result is a proof about the exact finite \(Sp_4(\mathbb F_3)\)-module constructed from the degree-4 obstruction. It is stronger than the earlier finite-probe evidence and does not rely on semisimplicity.

The result does **not** by itself prove that \(W\) is the known tilting module \(T(2,1)\), nor does it prove that the module canonically recovers Demuškin orientation. Those are separate identification and naturality questions.

The next research step is therefore literature/module identification:

1. verify \(U\cong L(2,0)\);
2. verify \(M\cong L(2,1)\);
3. verify whether \(W\cong T(2,1)\);
4. investigate the extension/Loewy structure and invariant duality;
5. only then return to the canonical-orientation question.
