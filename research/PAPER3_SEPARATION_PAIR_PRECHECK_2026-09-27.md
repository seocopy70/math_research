The naive argument

x^p in D_p and x^p not in D_{p+1}
therefore the two presentations first differ at degree p

is **not valid in the quotient Demushkin relation**. In G_p the relation identifies x1^p with [x1,x2]^{-1}, whose leading contribution is degree 2. Free-group p-power degree cannot be transferred directly to the quotient relation without checking the induced filtered relation.

Therefore this naive Step-1 justification is classified **FAIL / CLOSED** and must not be reused.

## S1 — TRUNCATED FILTERED WINDOW CALCULATION

Fix odd p and let F=<x,y> be the free pro-p group, with Zassenhaus filtration D_i(F). For q=p or p^2, let R_q be the closed normal subgroup generated by r_q=x^q[x,y]. The quotient-filtration identity gives

D_i(F/R_q)=D_i(F)R_q/R_q.

Hence
G_q/D_p(G_q) = F/(D_p(F)R_q).

Because x^p and x^(p^2) both lie in D_p(F), modulo D_p(F) the relation r_q=x^q[x,y] is equivalent to [x,y]=1 for both q=p and q=p^2. Therefore

G_p/D_p(G_p) = F/(D_p(F),[x,y]) = G_p2/D_p(G_p2)

as filtered quotients, not merely as abstract groups.

More generally, for every i<p, the induced filtration terms are

D_i(G_q)/D_p(G_q) = D_i(F)R_q/(D_p(F)R_q),

and R_p and R_p2 have the same image modulo D_p(F), namely the normal closure of [x,y]. Thus all filtration levels i=1,...,p-1 and the group law on the truncated quotient agree.

**S1 classification: PASS / CLOSED.**

Important boundary: this proves the specific W_p window isomorphism for the declared unmarked filtered-object definition. It does not use only graded vector-space q-blindness, and it does not prove anything about marked windows carrying extra presentation data. The pair therefore survives to S2.
