## 5. Intrinsicness boundary

The remaining issue is not the arithmetic recovery itself. It is the precise intrinsic definition of the exact truncated filtered relation carrier.

The chosen relator is not intrinsic: replacing it by a unit multiple rescales the carrier, and conjugating it changes the degree-3 component by the usual ([v,R])-gauge. Both are harmless for the recovery zero set, exactly as in the mod-9 projective jet.

What must be proved for a fully intrinsic theorem is that the exact truncated relation line is independent of the choice of minimal free presentation, with the induced transformations preserving the exact crossed-derivation zero set.

The existing degree-(2,3) projective gauge calculation strongly suggests the same mechanism, but it has only been established over the finite-field degree-3 jet. The exact-(mathbf Z_3) lift of that gauge lemma is therefore the remaining genuine mathematical gate.
