## 2. Exact relation with the Zassenhaus graded piece

For a free pro-p group \(F\), the p-Zassenhaus associated graded object is the free restricted Lie algebra on \(V=F/P_2(F)\). For Demuškin groups, the group is mild with respect to the p-Zassenhaus filtration and the graded group algebra is obtained from the free associative algebra by the ideal generated by the initial quadratic relator
\[
R=[X_1,X_2]+[X_3,X_4].
\]

At degree 4, the restricted structure adds no new independent generator beyond the ordinary free Lie degree-4 component:

- the degree-3 restricted powers \(X_i^{[3]}\) exist;
- bracketing \(X_i^{[3]}\) with a degree-1 generator gives
  \[
  [X_i^{[3]},X_j]=\operatorname{ad}(X_i)^3(X_j),
  \]
  which is an ordinary Lie degree-4 element;
- the next restricted p-powers have degree at least 6;
- hence the degree-4 primitive/restricted-Lie piece is represented by the ordinary \(L_4(F)\).

The restricted ideal generated by the quadratic relation has degree-4 part generated by the degree-2 relation followed by degree-2 brackets, i.e. the same degree-4 relation ideal used in the Paper 1 quotient. No degree-4 contribution comes from \(R^{[3]}\), whose filtration degree is 6.

Thus the natural map induced by the free presentation gives
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{gr}_4(G)=P_4/P_5
\;\cong\;
L_4(F)/(R)_4=Q_4.
}
\]

This identification is **natural at the level of the graded object**, not a coordinate-dependent accidental equality of two 45-dimensional vector spaces.

Independent dimension check:
\[
\dim P_4/P_5=45
\]
from the primary-source MRT formula for \(c_4\), while the Paper 1 calculation gives \(\dim Q_4=45\).
