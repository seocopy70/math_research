# E1 RESIDUAL-GAUGE LEMMA — 2026-09-19

## Target

Prove the degree-(2,3) residual-gauge statement for a free automorphism
\[
\alpha:F\to F
\]
that induces \(\mathrm{id}_G\), in the frozen rank-4 q=3 Demuškin presentation.

No finite computation.

## 1. Degree-one and degree-two behavior

Because \(\alpha\) induces the identity on \(G\), and the presentation is minimal,
\[
\alpha(X_i)=X_i+Q_i+O(3)
\]
in the associated graded Magnus/Lie expansion, with \(Q_i\in L_2\).

Since \(\alpha(x_i)x_i^{-1}\in R\), and the defining relation has initial degree 2 generated by
\[
R_2=[X_1,X_2]+[X_3,X_4],
\]
the degree-2 part of each correction is necessarily
\[
Q_i=c_iR_2
\]
for scalars \(c_i\in\mathbf F_3\).

This is the key one-relator input.

## 2. Effect on the quadratic relation

Apply \(\alpha\) to \(R_2\). Since \(R_2\) is homogeneous of degree 2, the first correction is the derivation obtained by replacing each \(X_i\) by \(Q_i\):
\[
\delta(R_2)
=
[Q_1,X_2]+[X_1,Q_2]+[Q_3,X_4]+[X_3,Q_4].
\]

Substituting \(Q_i=c_iR_2\),
\[
\delta(R_2)
=
[c_1R_2,X_2]+[X_1,c_2R_2]
+[c_3R_2,X_4]+[X_3,c_4R_2].
\]

Using antisymmetry,
\[
\delta(R_2)
=
[R_2,,
c_1X_2-c_2X_1+c_3X_4-c_4X_3].
\]

Thus
\[
\alpha(R_2)=R_2+[R_2,v]
\pmod{L_{\ge4}}
\]
for
\[
v=c_1X_2-c_2X_1+c_3X_4-c_4X_3\in V.
\]

So the only degree-3 change produced by an identity-on-G lift is exactly the relator-conjugation gauge.

## 3. Incorporating the defining relator class

The relation-module class of the defining relator may additionally be multiplied by a unit
\[
u\in\mathbf Z_3^\times.
\]
Hence at the degree-(2,3) jet level the transported pair has the form
\[
(R,P)\longmapsto
(uR,,uP+[v,R])
\]
after fixing the same graded convention, with the \([v,R]\) term understood in the degree-3 component.

For the mod-3 jet, every unit \(u\in\mathbf Z_3^\times\) reduces to \(\pm1\); the common scaling is precisely the projective ambiguity already built into \(J_3=\langle(R,P)\rangle\).

## 4. Effect on the recovery functional

For \(f\in V^*=H^1(G,\mathbf F_3)\),
\[
f([v,R])=0,
\]
because a degree-one functional annihilates brackets.

Therefore
\[
\Theta_{uR,uP+[v,R]}(\lambda)(f)
=
u\Theta_{R,P}(\lambda)(f).
\]

Hence the zero set
\[
\{\lambda:\Theta_J(\lambda)=0\}
\]
is unchanged.

This is exactly the invariance required for orientation recovery; canonical normalization of the relation-module generator is unnecessary.

## 5. Scope and caveat

The proof uses the frozen one-relator degree-2 fact that every degree-2 correction coming from an identity-on-G lift lies in the one-dimensional relation line \(\langle R_2\rangle\).

It does NOT prove a universal statement for arbitrary pro-p groups or arbitrary multi-relator presentations.

It also does not by itself prove that the entire projective jet construction is presentation-free in every higher filtered degree; only the degree-(2,3) ambiguity relevant to \(\Theta\) is controlled.

## Decision

**E1 residual-gauge lemma: PASS at the degree-(2,3) level relevant to Theta.**

Consequences:
- full relation-module generator invariance: still FAIL;
- projective degree-(2,3) jet invariance relevant to Theta: PASS;
- E1 full intrinsicness: substantially advanced, but should be recorded as **PASS conditional on the standard minimal one-relator presentation facts and the frozen filtered convention**.
