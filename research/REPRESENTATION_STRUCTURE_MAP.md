## Outer quotient

Define

\[
E=W/U.
\]

Then

\[
\dim E=35
\]

\[
0\to M_{25}\to E_{35}\to Sym^2(V)_{10}\to0.

\[
Hom_H(Sym^2(V),E)=0.
