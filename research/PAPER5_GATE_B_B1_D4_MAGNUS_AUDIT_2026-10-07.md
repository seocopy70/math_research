# Paper 5 Gate B — B1 D4 Magnus Audit — 2026-10-07

## Scope

Active load-bearing branch: pro-p diagonal realization. A2 is already PASS / CLOSED and is not reopened. This audit tests only the first possible obstruction in
E_a = x^{ap}[x^a,y]z^{-ap}
for a in F_p^×.

## Computation

Work in the completed mod-p Magnus algebra
T = F_p<<X,Y,Z>>,
x -> 1+X, y -> 1+Y, z -> 1+Z,
with q=ap.

For p >= 5, the degree-4 part of x^{q}-1 and z^{-q}-1 is zero modulo p, because q is divisible by p and 4<p. Equivalently, for 1<=j<p, binom(ap,j) is divisible by p.

Hence the degree-4 component of E_a is exactly the degree-4 component of [x^a,y].

The initial Zassenhaus relation is [X,Y] in degree 2. The degree-4 Magnus component of [x^a,y] lies in the two-sided associative ideal generated by [X,Y]: [x^a,y] is a product of conjugates of [x,y], and in the associated graded its positive-degree Magnus components therefore vanish after quotienting by the initial relation ideal. Direct symbolic reduction confirms this.

For the representative a=2, the raw associative degree-4 word expression is nonzero before quotienting, but its class modulo the degree-4 relation ideal is zero. The same reduction is structural for every a.

A direct finite-field word-space check was also performed for p=5: the degree-4 component of E_a lies in the degree-4 component of the Magnus relation ideal, so its quotient class is zero.

For p=3, degree-4 receives p-power contributions and is exceptional in bookkeeping. A direct word-space check nevertheless also places the computed degree-4 E_a component in the degree-4 relation ideal for the tested a=2 case. This is LOCAL only and does not constitute the general p=3 statement.

## Conclusion

For p >= 5,
[E_a]_4 = 0 in the degree-4 associated graded quotient.

Thus the proposed D4 obstruction does NOT occur. In fact the B1 defect survives past D4; the next potentially load-bearing layer is D5 (more precisely, the first nonzero residual class after the relation-ideal quotient must be identified before choosing the correction degree).

This invalidates the tentative B3 reduction
L_2 ->^{ad_{z^a}} L_3
as the decisive obstruction at degree 4. Since the D4 class vanishes, no surjectivity test is needed at that layer.
