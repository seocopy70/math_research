# A3-4-11 — Degree-5 obstruction compression into the free Lie space

Date: 2026-09-16

## Purpose

A3-4-10 verified that the A3-4-9 module/extension intertwiner
\(X:W_{45}\to W_d\), fixing the common \(K\), is not compatible with the ambient degree-5 bracket. The obstruction was measured in the 1024-dimensional associative degree-5 word space.

A3-4-11 asks whether this obstruction is genuinely a free-Lie phenomenon, and then packages it representation-theoretically.

## Exact questions

1. Construct the actual free Lie space \(L_5\subset V^{\otimes5}\) and verify \(\dim L_5=204\).
2. Project the four degree-5 bracket discrepancy components from the associative realization to \(L_5\).
3. Check that the projection is lossless and preserves the obstruction rank.
4. Treat the discrepancy as a map
\[
\Phi:W_{45}\to \operatorname{Hom}(V,L_5)
\]
and test \(Sp_4(\mathbb F_3)\)-equivariance.
5. Determine whether the image is an embedded copy of \(W_{45}\) by injectivity.
6. As a local relation check, compute the degree-5 span generated by bracketing the verified degree-4 relation space \((R)_4\) with the generators, and measure its intersection with the obstruction image.

## Important limitation

The relation calculation is explicitly only
\[
\langle[(R)_4,X_i]\rangle\subseteq (R)_5.
\]
It is **not** being identified automatically with the full degree-5 relation ideal. A full degree-5 ideal calculation would be a separate experiment.

## Expected interpretation

If the rank remains 45 after projection, then A3-4-10's obstruction is not an artifact of the 1024-dimensional tensor realization. If equivariance also holds, its image is an \(Sp_4(\mathbb F_3)\)-module isomorphic to \(W_{45}\). This would locate the q-sensitive distinction at the level of the ambient degree-raising Lie bracket, rather than at the abstract 45-dimensional module/extension level.

This still does **not** establish canonical orientation or identify the obstruction with the candidate \(\lambda\pmod3\). Those remain later questions.
