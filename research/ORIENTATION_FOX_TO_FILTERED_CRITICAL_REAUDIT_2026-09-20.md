## Executive verdict

The recent Fox results are real, but their significance must be stated more narrowly than the previous enthusiastic wording.

What is genuinely closed is:

\[

\iff
\text{universal crossed-derivation descent}
\iff
J_r(\chi)=0

for the standard Demuškin orientation theorem and the fixed Fox convention, together with exact presentation covariance of the tested Fox construction.

\Longrightarrow
\chi:G\to\mathbf Z_3^\times.

The Fox branch now supplies an exact characteristic-zero *target condition*. It does not yet prove that the intrinsic filtered tower contains enough information to reconstruct that target condition.

\[

It should not be advertised as discovering the orientation itself. The exact value

\chi(x_2)=(1-3)^{-1}

## 2. Second critical correction: the mod-9 agreement is strong but only first-order

With
\[
A=1+3a,\ B=1+3b,
\]
the exact equation
\[
F_1=B(1+A)+A^2=0
\]
becomes
\[
F_1/3=1+2b+3(a+a^2+ab).
\]

Together with A=1, the exact equation is simply
\[
1+2b=0
\]
over \(\mathbf Z_3\).

Thus the mod-9 reduction recovers only
\[
1+2b\equiv0\pmod3.
\]

This proves exact compatibility of the first nontrivial digit, but it does **not** by itself prove that the entire higher filtered tower is the successive associated-graded expansion of the Fox equation.

For this particular frozen q=3 normal form, after the already-forced A=1, the higher terms vanish identically and the exact lift is trivial Hensel continuation. That is a special simplification of this relation, not yet a general theorem about filtered Demuškin groups.

## 3. Third critical correction: the full filtered-tower statement remains vulnerable to hidden redefinition

The project has already caught a previous circularity: a tower was at risk of being defined as exactly the data needed to evaluate the crossed-derivation condition.

The same audit must therefore be applied here.

A legitimate tower \(J_n\) must be defined independently from:

- the exact Fox row;
- the character \(\chi\);
- the crossed-derivation coefficient functions;
- or a quotient explicitly designed to retain their evaluations.

Only after the tower is independently defined may one prove a factorization
\[
J_n\longmapsto \chi_n.
\]

Otherwise “full filtered tower recovers chi” is again a tautological encoding.

## 4. Fourth critical correction: exact q=3 is not universal

For the fixed relation
\[
x_1^3[x_1,x_2][x_3,x_4]
\]
the exact Fox equation collapses to
\[
A=C=D=1,\qquad 1+2B=0.
\]

Hence one exact coefficient equation already contains infinitely many 3-adic digits.

This is not a contradiction with the bounded-degree/finite-information no-go theorem. An exact \(\mathbf Z_3\)-coefficient object contains infinitely much information.

Therefore the genuine unresolved question is not “can q=3 be solved exactly?” It plainly can. The question is:

> Can the exact coefficient equation be recovered from a finite, presentation-independent filtered object, without importing the coefficient equation itself?

The current evidence does not yet answer this.

## 5. Fifth critical correction: the Nielsen tests are validation, not a general intrinsicity theorem

The two explicit Nielsen tests are useful and the Fox chain rule gives the general mechanism. But a publication-grade intrinsicity theorem still needs:

1. the precise category of admissible minimal pro-3 presentations;
2. the exact coefficient-ring/completion;
3. left/right Fox convention;
4. relator-unit and conjugation gauge;
5. proof that all presentation changes are generated by the stated moves in the relevant pro-p category;
6. compatibility with continuous completion.

Therefore the status should be “structurally justified, explicitly stress-tested,” not “proved solely by the two tests.”

## 6. Decisive next attack

The next step should be a **non-circular finite-level factorization theorem**, not another Fox computation.

Define independently, from the filtered relation itself, a finite-level object \(K_n\) that contains only:

- the Zassenhaus filtered relation jet through the necessary degree;
- its canonical extension/lift data modulo \(3^n\);
- no reference to \(\chi\), Fox derivatives, crossed derivations, or their evaluations.

Then prove or disprove:
\[
K_n\Longrightarrow
\{\rho\in(\mathbf Z/3^n)^4:
J_r(\rho)=0\}.
\]

For q=3 the target set is the singleton
\[
(1,(-2)^{-1},1,1)\pmod{3^n}.
\]

The decisive test is whether the map from K_n to this singleton can be derived from intrinsic filtered operations and standard coefficient arithmetic, rather than by defining K_n to remember the target.

## 7. Stronger attack: compare q=3 and q=3^s

A particularly sharp falsification test is the family
\[
G_{3^s}=\langle x_i\mid
x_1^{3^s}[x_1,x_2][x_3,x_4]\rangle.
\]

The exact orientation is
\[
\chi_{3^s}(x_2)=(1-3^s)^{-1}.
\]

Any proposed filtered factorization should reproduce the fact that the first nontrivial coefficient layer moves to Zassenhaus degree \(3^s\).

Therefore:

- if the proposed finite-level construction sees the change only because it was explicitly told the power layer, it is not a universal bounded-degree carrier;
- if it derives the change canonically from the filtered relation jet, that is genuine progress;
- if fixed low-degree data cannot see it, the existing no-go theorem applies.

This family is the correct adversarial test because it separates true filtered information from exact coefficient information.
