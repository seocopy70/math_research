# PAPER 4 — q>0 ZASSENHAUS GRADED STRESS AUDIT — 2026-10-02

## Candidate

For odd p, q_D=p^a>0, consider the stress presentation
G_{s,a}=<z,x_1,...,x_d | z^{p^s}=x_1^{p^a}[x_1,x_2]...[x_{d-1},x_d]>.

## Initial-form calculation

In the p-Zassenhaus filtration, a commutator has degree 2 while z^{p^s} has degree p^s and x_1^{p^a} has degree p^a. Since p^a,p^s >=3 for odd p and a,s>=1, the degree-2 initial form of the defining relator is solely
rho=[X_1,X_2]+[X_3,X_4]+...+[X_{d-1},X_d],
up to sign/unit convention. It is independent of both a and s.

Thus the first associated-graded relation layer is completely blind to the extension depth.

## Mildness check

Let U be the span of the x_i classes in H^1(G,F_p), and V the span of the z class. The quadratic initial relation gives a nonzero symplectic cup-product form on U and the z-class is radical at degree two. Hence U cup U surjects onto H^2(G,F_p), while V cup V=0.

By the Schmidt criterion as recorded in Gärtner's treatment of mild pro-p groups, this implies mildness (for the odd-p setting under discussion), hence cd_p G=2. For a mild group the graded completed group algebra is presented by the ideal generated by the initial form(s) of the defining relation(s). Therefore the standard p-Zassenhaus associated graded algebra is determined by the same quadratic rho for every s.

## Result

- candidate initial-form blindness to s: PASS / CLOSED;
- standard mod-p Zassenhaus associated graded as a detector of s: FAIL / CLOSED;
- full finite Zassenhaus window: still OPEN — a finite quotient contains more integral information than its associated graded;
- untwisted E2 homological layer for q>0 deep tail: FAIL / CLOSED (previous correction);
- genuinely integral/nonabelian Magnus or relation-module data beyond associated graded: OPEN / LOAD-BEARING.

## Boundary

This does NOT prove that finite windows cannot recover s. It proves only that any such recovery cannot factor solely through the ordinary mod-p associated graded Zassenhaus object.

The next authorized question is therefore narrower:
Can an intrinsic, gauge-invariant integral p-adic relation/Magnus truncation, strictly richer than the associated graded, factor through some finite window and distinguish s>a?

No new arbitrary carrier is to be introduced before that factorization/gauge test is formulated.
