## 1. Position

O2-4 established that the absolute obstruction image
\[
O_\tau=\operatorname{Im}D_\tau
\]
is not invariant under the three admissible normalized transports. The exact pairwise join ranks were 20 for distinct 10-dimensional images, so the three images are not equal.

O2-5 asks a narrower structural question: because the admissible transports form the affine family
\[
\tau_b=\tau(I+bN),\qquad b\in\mathbb F_3,
\]
does the *variation direction* of the obstruction define a stable 10-dimensional object?

This is not a replacement for O2-4. It tests a different object.

## 2. Admissibility / coordinate audit

The earlier O2-5 implementation attempts mixed coefficient coordinates with the 256-dimensional degree-4 ambient coordinates. Those checks were rejected and corrected.

The final implementation uses the same-coordinate ambient comparison:
\[
I_{W,\mathrm{coeff}}=Q_{W45}^{-1}I_{\mathrm{coord}},
\]
\[
I_{\mathrm{ambient}}^{(W)}=W I_{W,\mathrm{coeff}},
\]
\[
T_b^{\mathrm{amb}}=W_d\tau_bQ_{W45}^{-1},
\]
\[
I_{\mathrm{ambient}}^{(\tau_b)}=T_b^{\mathrm{amb}}I_{W,\mathrm{coeff}}.
\]
The B1 pointwise condition is then tested by equality of these two ambient-coordinate matrices.

The number 256 is therefore the dimension of the degree-4 ambient coordinate space (\(4^4\)); it is not being treated as the dimension of \(W\).

## 4. Critical interpretation

The result is useful, but its logical strength must be stated narrowly.

### What O2-5 establishes

It establishes that, **within the fixed B1 admissible affine family** \(\{\tau_0,\tau_1,\tau_2\}\), the change in the obstruction map has a rank-10 image and that this variation image is H-stable.

### What O2-5 does NOT establish

It does **not** establish that \(\Delta O\) is canonical independently of the choice of base transport \(\tau_0\), independently of a different admissible affine family, or intrinsically determined by the filtration alone.

It also does not establish that \(\Delta O\cong U\) canonically, even though both have dimension 10 and \(\Delta O\) is H-stable. A module isomorphism still requires an explicit construction or an appropriate intrinsic characterization.

Finally, it does not distinguish \(q=3\) from \(q=\infty\) and does not recover the orientation character \(\chi\).

## 5. Mathematical meaning of the affine identity

Since
\[
D_h(v)=\tau([v,X_h])-[\tau(v),X_h]
\]
is linear in \(\tau\), and
\[
\tau_b=\tau+b(\tau N),
\]
we have
\[
D_b=D_0+bD(\tau N).
\]
Writing
\[
\Delta D=D(\tau N),
\]
therefore gives
\[
D_1-D_0=\Delta D,
\qquad
D_2-D_1=\Delta D,
\qquad
D_2-D_0=2\Delta D
\]
over \(\mathbb F_3\).

Thus the new 10-dimensional object is best described at this checkpoint as a **transport-variation module**, not yet as a canonical obstruction module.

The next experiment should not simply repeat the rank calculation. It should test whether the construction of \(\Delta O\) itself survives the remaining freedom in the choice of admissible transport/basepoint.
