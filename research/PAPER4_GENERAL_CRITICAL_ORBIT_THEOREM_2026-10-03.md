# PAPER 4 — GENERAL CRITICAL-WINDOW QUOTIENT-MAP ORBIT THEOREM
## 2026-10-03

### Question

For the critical finite window
\[
W_n=G/D_n(G),\qquad n=p^s+1,
\]
with
\[
G=\langle z,x_1,\ldots,x_d\mid z^{p^s}=r_D\rangle,
\qquad
Q_n=D/D_n(D),
\]
consider admissible epimorphisms
\[
\pi:W_n\twoheadrightarrow Q_n
\]
whose induced \(H^1\)-kernel is the intrinsic cup-radical line generated by \(\bar z\).

Does the W10 one-orbit phenomenon persist for arbitrary odd \(p\), \(s\ge1\), and the declared Demushkin rank?

### 1. General p^s-power correction lemma

Let \(A\) be a complete associative algebra over \(\mathbf F_p\), with augmentation ideal \(I\). If \(a,b\in I\) and
\[
a-b\in I^2,
\]
then for every \(s\ge1\),
\[
\boxed{a^{p^s}\equiv b^{p^s}\pmod{I^{p^s+1}}.}
\]

Proof by induction on \(s\). For \(s=1\), write \(a=b+d\) with \(d\in I^2\). Every mixed word in \((b+d)^p-b^p\) contains at least one \(d\), hence has degree at least
\[
(p-1)\cdot1+2=p+1,
\]
while \(d^p\) has degree \(2p\ge p+1\). Thus
\[
a^p-b^p\in I^{p+1}.
\]

Assume
\[
a^{p^k}=b^{p^k}+E_k,
\qquad E_k\in I^{p^k+1}.
\]
Then \(b^{p^k}\in I^{p^k}\), and every mixed word in
\[
(b^{p^k}+E_k)^p-(b^{p^k})^p
\]
contains at least one \(E_k\) and \(p-1\) factors \(b^{p^k}\), so has degree at least
\[
(p^k+1)+(p-1)p^k=p^{k+1}+1.
\]
The pure \(E_k^p\) term has even larger degree. Hence
\[
E_{k+1}\in I^{p^{k+1}+1}.
\]
This proves the claim.

### 2. Group-theoretic radical shear

Let \(c\in D_2(W_n)\), and choose a lift \(\tilde c\in D_2(F)\) in the free pro-\(p\) presentation group. Under Magnus,
\[
M(z)=1+Z,
\qquad
M(z\tilde c)-1\in I^2.
\]
Applying the lemma with \(a=M(z\tilde c)-1\) and \(b=M(z)-1\) gives
\[
(z\tilde c)^{p^s}\equiv z^{p^s}\pmod{D_{p^s+1}(F)}.
\]
Therefore, on \(W_n\),
\[
(zc)^{p^s}=z^{p^s}=r_D.
\]
Thus
\[
\alpha_c(z)=zc,
\qquad
\alpha_c(x_i)=x_i
\]
preserves the defining relation and induces an endomorphism of \(W_n\).

It induces the identity on
\[
W_n/D_2(W_n),
\]
which is the Frattini quotient of the finite p-group \(W_n\). Burnside's basis theorem therefore implies
\[
\boxed{\alpha_c\in\operatorname{Aut}(W_n).}
\]

### 3. Every admissible quotient map normalizes to the canonical map

Let
\[
\pi:W_n\twoheadrightarrow Q_n
\]
be admissible, so
\[
\pi(z)\in D_2(Q_n).
\]
Put
\[
x_i'=\pi(x_i).
\]
The induced map on \(H^1\) has kernel exactly the radical line \(\langle\bar z\rangle\), hence the images \(\bar x_i'\) form a basis of
\[
Q_n/D_2(Q_n)\cong H^1(D,\mathbf F_p)^\vee
\]
(in the standard dual identification).

Since \(\pi(z)\in D_2(Q_n)\), one has
\[
\pi(z)^{p^s}=1,
\]
because
\[
D_2(Q_n)^{p^s}\subseteq D_{2p^s}(Q_n)=1
\]
as \(2p^s\ge p^s+1=n\).

Applying \(\pi\) to the defining relation gives
\[
r_D(x_1',\ldots,x_d')=1.
\]
Therefore the assignment
\[
x_i\longmapsto x_i'
\]
defines a surjective endomorphism
\[
\varphi:Q_n\to Q_n.
\]
Since \(Q_n\) is finite, \(\varphi\) is an automorphism.

Postcompose by \(\varphi^{-1}\). Then
\[
\pi'(x_i)=x_i,
\qquad
\pi'(z)=c
\]
for some \(c\in D_2(Q_n)\).

### 4. Radical-shear transitivity

Choose a lift \(\tilde c\in D_2(W_n)\) of \(c\). The canonical quotient \(\pi_0\) satisfies
\[
\pi_0\circ\alpha_{\tilde c}(z)=c,
\qquad
\pi_0\circ\alpha_{\tilde c}(x_i)=x_i.
\]
Hence
\[
\pi'=\pi_0\circ\alpha_{\tilde c}.
\]
Undoing the normalization gives
\[
\boxed{
\pi
=
\varphi\circ\pi_0\circ\alpha_{\tilde c}.
}
\]

Thus every admissible quotient map lies in a single
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Aut}(W_n)\times\operatorname{Aut}(Q_n)
\text{-orbit}.
}
\]

### 5. Consequences

The W10 phenomenon is therefore not accidental. It is a formal consequence of the critical depth \(n=p^s+1\), the radical-line condition, and the p^s-power congruence.

For the declared stress-family critical windows:

- general critical-window quotient-map orbit transitivity:
  **PASS / CLOSED**;
- same-window separation by different admissible quotient-map orbits:
  **FAIL / CLOSED** at this level;
- relative split/non-split Boolean is constant on the admissible realization orbit:
  **PASS / LOCAL**;
- general unmarked reconstruction of a unique marked quotient map:
  **FAIL / CLOSED as a uniqueness target at the map level**;
- intrinsic/coarsest realization from \(W_n\) alone:
  **OPEN / LOAD-BEARING**.

The last two statements must not be conflated. Orbit transitivity says that the marked map is unique only up to the natural two-sided gauge group; it does not yet construct a canonical quotient-map object, nor prove that the Boolean obstruction is computable from an explicitly defined coarsest intrinsic finite category.

### 6. Logical boundary

This theorem is about the critical windows \(n=p^s+1\) and the declared admissible class. It does not prove:

1. a universal theorem for arbitrary free-by-Demushkin extensions;
2. recovery of a literal marked quotient map;
3. a coarsest intrinsic realization category;
4. the unresolved \(a=1\) relative survival threshold;
5. any reopening of the frozen degree-5/scalar/norm/RAAG branches.
