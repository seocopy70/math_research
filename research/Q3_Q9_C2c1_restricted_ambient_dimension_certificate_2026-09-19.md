## 2. Existing project convention

The repository's degree-3 construction explicitly uses the tensor realization of the free restricted Lie algebra over F3, with

X1^[3] represented by the associative word X1 X1 X1.

The repository also verifies the restricted identity

[X1^[3], X2] = ad(X1)^3(X2).

Therefore the degree-9 ambient must not be replaced by the ordinary free Lie space L9 alone.

## 3. Ordinary free Lie dimension

For r=4 generators, Witt's formula gives

dim L_n = (1/n) sum_{d|n} mu(d) 4^(n/d).

For n=9,

dim L9 = (1/9)(4^9 - 4^3)
       = (262144 - 64)/9
       = 29120.

## 4. Restricted p-power contributions in degree 9

The characteristic is p=3.

Degree 9 has two relevant p-power layers:

### 4.1 First restricted cube

Elements of degree 3 can be cubed by the restricted operation:

L3 -> degree 9.

Since

dim L3 = (1/3)(4^3 - 4)
       = 20,

this contributes a 20-dimensional restricted p-power layer.

### 4.2 Second restricted cube

The degree-1 generators can be cubed twice:

L1 -> L3^[3] -> degree 9,

equivalently the degree-9 restricted p-power layer generated by the four degree-1 generators.

Since

dim L1 = 4,

this contributes a 4-dimensional layer.

## 5. Restricted ambient dimension

Under the standard free restricted Lie algebra grading convention used here, these layers give

L9^res = L9 ⊕ L3^[3] ⊕ L1^[9]

at the level of the degree-9 vector-space decomposition.

Hence

dim L9^res
 = 29120 + 20 + 4
 = 29144.

Therefore the degree-9 restricted ambient dimension is

**29144**.

## 6. Important qualification

It is **not** being presented as a number already hard-coded or directly printed by an existing repository script.

Accordingly:

- dim L3 = 20 and dim L1 = 4: verified mathematically;
