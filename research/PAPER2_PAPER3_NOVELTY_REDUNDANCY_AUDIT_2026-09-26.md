# Paper 2 + Paper 3 — novelty / redundancy audit — 2026-09-26

## Question

Determine whether the former Paper 3 corpus supplies an independent publication-level
result after absorption into Paper 2, or whether it is better treated as
applications/consequences of Paper 2 plus the preceding recognition theorem.

## Findings

### A. What is already the Paper 2 contribution

The independent central contribution remains the exact affine-category window
[
n_{\mathrm{aff}}(k)=p^{k-1}+1
]
with sharpness for all odd (p), all (f\ge1), and all even (d\ge2), including
the rank-two boundary and without requiring a surjective orientation component.

This is the theorem that gives Paper 2 its independent mathematical center.

### B. What the former Paper 3 adds

The absorbed material contributes:

1. uniformity of the same affine window on finite free pro-(p) products;
2. compatibility of Zassenhaus truncation with free coproducts;
3. the finite-depth (f_i<k) versus (f_i\ge k) parameter profile;
4. an explicit heterogeneous composite example;
5. blockwise application of the Paper 1 Kummer selector in the rank-four
   (q=3) class.

These are mathematically useful consequences and applications. They do not
replace the central theorem with a second independent theorem whose novelty
is logically separate from Paper 2.

### C. Redundancy if kept as a standalone Paper 3

As a standalone paper in its current form, the former Paper 3 would have to
present the Paper 2 factorization theorem as an imported main tool and then
demonstrate applications. Its novelty would therefore depend on whether one
of the applications could be elevated into a genuinely new general theorem.

The current corpus does not require that extra claim. Keeping it separate
would make the publication boundary less clean and invite the referee
question: why is this not an applications section of Paper 2?

Therefore the current absorption is the cleaner publication structure.

### D. Does absorption destroy novelty?

No. The applications do not supply the novelty of Paper 2; they increase its
scope and interpretability. The central theorem remains independently stated,
proved, and sharp.

### E. Literature boundary

The audited literature establishes the canonical Demuškin orientation and
Kummerian/cyclotomic framework, as well as substantial Zassenhaus and
free-product technology. The exact theorem claimed here is deliberately
described as a narrow conditional novelty boundary rather than a priority
claim.

In particular, Efrat's work already connects Zassenhaus terms with
representation kernels, so the manuscript must not present the generic
functorial factorization principle as new. The claimed contribution is the
specific affine target-filtration calculation together with sharp Demuškin
witnesses and the resulting exact depth. The literature audit did not locate
that exact combined statement verbatim.

## Decision

**Paper 3 should be absorbed into Paper 2 for the present submission.**

The former Paper 3 source should be retained in the repository as research
history/application source material, not treated as a separately submitted
publication candidate.

A future independent Paper 3 would require a new theorem whose mathematical
content is not merely an application of the Paper 2 window and Paper 1
recognition result.
