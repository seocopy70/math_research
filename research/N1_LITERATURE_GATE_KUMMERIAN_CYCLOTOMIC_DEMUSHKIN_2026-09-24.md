## 2. Labute 1967 — exact boundary

The decisive Labute result used by later papers is Proposition 6 together with Theorem 4.

Methodological content:
- A rank-one coefficient module I(chi) attached to an orientation chi is used.
- Surjectivity of the reduction maps H^1(G,I/p^i I) -> H^1(G,I/p I) is the key lifting condition.
- The orientation is characterized/selected by a cohomological crossed-derivation lifting condition.
- The framework also supports the standard classification by rank and orientation image/invariant q.

Boundary for this project:
Labute is a full-group theorem. It does not state that the orientation can be reconstructed from the bare finite quotient Q_k=G/P_{k+1} by a q-blind predicate, nor does it prove a natural factorization of the orientation-selection functional through Q_k.

## 3. Efrat–Quadrelli 2019 — exact relevant results

### 3.1 Definition/input
A cyclotomic pro-p pair is (G,theta), with theta:G -> 1+p Z_p in the torsion-free setup. The canonical subgroup K(G,theta) is generated by h^{-theta(g)} g h g^{-1}, for h in ker(theta). The pair is Kummerian when ker(theta)/K(G,theta) is free abelian pro-p.

### 3.2 Cohomological characterization
Theorem 7.1: for finitely generated torsion-free cyclotomic pro-p pairs, Kummerianity is equivalent to surjectivity, for every n>=1, of
H^1(G,Z/p^n(1)_theta) -> H^1(G,Z/p(1)_theta).

This is very close to the lifting predicate used in B2.

### 3.3 Labute lifting formulation
Proposition 7.3, attributed explicitly to Labute Proposition 6, gives equivalence between:
(a) surjectivity of the H^1 coefficient reduction maps;
(b) every prescribed assignment on a minimal generating set extends to a cocycle with Z/p^n(1)_theta coefficients;
(c) every Z_p-valued assignment on a minimal generating set extends to a Z_p(1)_theta cocycle.

### 3.4 Demushkin uniqueness
Theorem 7.6: for every torsion-free Demushkin pro-p group G there is a unique continuous theta:G -> 1+pZ_p such that (G,theta) is Kummerian.

### 3.5 Novelty boundary
The abstract selection principle
candidate orientation theta + all-level H^1 lifting/Kummerianity => canonical orientation
is already known.

Therefore B2 cannot claim novelty merely for observing that the correct orientation is the unique orientation for which the Kummerian lifting property holds on the full group.

The distinct B2 question is whether this selection principle can be reconstructed as a natural finite predicate of Q_k=G/P_{k+1}, without importing q/chi/classification and with an explicit factorization proof. Efrat–Quadrelli do not prove that finite-window factorization statement.

## 4. Quadrelli–Weigel 2020 — exact relevant results

### 4.1 Stronger notion: cyclotomic orientation
A p-orientation theta:G->Z_p^x is cyclotomic if, for every open subgroup U<=G and every k,n>=1, the maps
H^k(U,Z_p(k)/p^n) -> H^k(U,Z_p(k)/p)
are surjective.

This is stronger in quantification than the single-group H^1 Kummerian condition used in B2.

### 4.2 Poincare duality and canonical orientation
For a p-Poincare duality group, the dualizing module produces a canonical dualizing p-orientation. The paper proves that for dimension 2 the dualizing orientation is cyclotomic; consequently, for Demushkin pro-p groups the canonical dualizing orientation is cyclotomic and the oriented group is Bloch-Kato.

### 4.3 Boundary
The 2020 paper confirms an intrinsic/cohomological route from the PD2 dualizing object to the canonical orientation, but does not construct a q-blind finite filtered carrier J_k or a natural predicate on Q_k=G/P_{k+1} that selects theta mod p^k.

Therefore it does not close B2.

## 5. Quadrelli–Weigel 2022 — exact relevant results

### 5.1 Proposition 2.6
For torsion-free oriented pro-ell groups, the following are equivalent, including:
(i) H^1(G,Z_ell(1)/ell^n) -> H^1(G,F_ell) is surjective for every n;
(ii) ker(theta)/K_theta(G) is free abelian pro-ell;
(iii) the quotient by K_theta is theta-abelian;
(iv) K_theta is isolated in ker(theta), equivalently I_theta=K_theta;
(v) H^2(G,Z_ell(1)) is torsion-free;
(vi) I_theta(G) is contained in Phi(G).

Thus Kummerianity has several equivalent intrinsic formulations, but these are formulations for the oriented pro-ell group (G,theta), not a bare finite quotient Q_k.

### 5.2 Demushkin uniqueness
Section 5.1 states that a Demushkin group has a distinguished orientation induced by the dualizing module and that this is the only orientation completing G into a Kummerian oriented pro-ell group. Theorem 5.1 then proves the Bogomolov–Positselski property for the canonical orientation.

### 5.3 Proposition 5.2
For a finitely generated one-relator pro-ell group with quadratic cohomology, if the trivial orientation is Kummerian, then the group has the Bogomolov–Positselski property. This is a structural consequence of Kummerianity/quadratic cohomology, not an orientation-recovery-from-finite-quotient theorem.

## 6. Direct comparison with B2

B2 candidate at level k:
K_k(rho): H^1(Q_k, Z/3^k(rho)) -> H^1(Q_k,F_3) is surjective,
where Q_k=G/P_{k+1}, rho is a candidate orientation modulo 3^k.

Known literature:
- Labute: full-G lifting criterion selects the canonical orientation.
- Efrat–Quadrelli: Kummerianity of a given oriented full pro-p group is equivalent to all-level H^1 lifting, and Demushkin groups have a unique such orientation.
- Quadrelli–Weigel 2020: cyclotomicity is an all-open-subgroup/all-degree strengthening; PD2 dualizing orientation is cyclotomic.
- Quadrelli–Weigel 2022: Kummerianity has equivalent intrinsic cohomological/group-theoretic formulations; Demushkin canonical orientation is the unique Kummerian completion.

None of these four papers proves the specific finite-window factorization statement:
full-G Kummer/lifting predicate factors naturally through Q_k=G/P_{k+1}, for candidate rho, with q absent from the definition.

The finite quotient also creates a separate existence problem: a mod-3^k cocycle constructed on G must annihilate P_{k+1} before it becomes a cocycle on Q_k. The full-G lifting theorem does not supply this annihilation automatically.

## 10. Reusable methodology extracted from the literature

Labute:
object = rank-one coefficient module attached to orientation;
input = full Demushkin group + candidate orientation;
obstruction = relation/crossed-derivation lifting;
verification = prescribed generator values extend to cocycles;
boundary = full-G criterion, not finite quotient factorization.

Efrat–Quadrelli:
object = K(G,theta) / Kummerian pair;
input = oriented pro-p pair;
invariance = morphisms of cyclotomic pairs and functorial K;
obstruction = H^1 lifting / cocycle extension;
verification = cohomology and cocycle equivalences;
boundary = orientation is an input, not reconstructed from a bare finite quotient.

Quadrelli–Weigel 2020:
object = cyclotomic p-orientation / dualizing orientation;
input = profinite group plus orientation or PD structure;
invariance = open subgroups and morphisms of oriented groups;
obstruction = torsion in twisted top cohomology / failure of lifting;
verification = PD duality and cohomology;
boundary = no finite Q_k orientation selector.

Quadrelli–Weigel 2022:
object = Kummerian oriented pro-ell group and maximal theta-abelian quotient;
input = oriented group;
invariance = oriented-group morphisms;
obstruction = K_theta, I_theta, H^2 torsion, transgression;
verification = Hochschild–Serre / quadratic cohomology;
boundary = does not provide Q_k reconstruction.
