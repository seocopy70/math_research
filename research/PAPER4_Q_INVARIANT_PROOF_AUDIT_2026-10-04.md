## Target

For the stress family
G_{u,a} = <z,x_1,...,x_d | z^{p^u}=r>,
with z-free r and m(r) <= p^s, the proposed unmarked separator is

Q(W): there exists a generating tuple (g,H), with H generated by d elements,
such that 1 != g^{p^s} in H.

The p=3,s=1,r=x^3 computation reports Q(W_s)=true and Q(W_t)=false.

## Proof reduction

The group-level implication
g^{p^s} in H, g^{p^s} != 1
passes to the first nonzero Zassenhaus layer. The associated graded object is a restricted Lie algebra, and Hall–Petrescu supplies the compatibility
D_m^p <= D_{pm}, [D_m,D_n] <= D_{m+n}.

For the desired general proof, the essential remaining statement is the following restricted-Lie separation lemma:

(RL_s) Let L be the free restricted Lie algebra on V and let I be the restricted ideal generated by the initial relation r-bar, with r-bar supported in the x-variables and degree <= p^s. For every decomposition V=<v> direct-sum U corresponding to a generating tuple (g,H), if v^[p^s] is nonzero in L/I, then v^[p^s] is not contained in the restricted subalgebra generated by U.

If (RL_s) holds and the passage from H to gr(H) is written carefully, Q(W_t)=false follows for all t>s. For W_s, the defining relation gives a nonzero p^s-power in the distinguished z-direction for the control case, yielding Q(W_s)=true.

## Current proof status

(RL_s) is NOT YET PROVED. The phrase “Hall–Petrescu third-order term prevents cancellation” is insufficient as a general proof. The correct route appears to require a PBW/normal-form argument in the restricted enveloping algebra, using that a restricted subalgebra of a free restricted Lie algebra is free and that homogeneous reduced generators remain independent.

Relevant literature support:
- Zassenhaus filtration compatibility and Hall–Petrescu consequences: recent characteristic-growth exposition.
- Free restricted Lie algebra PBW realization and freeness of restricted subalgebras: Bryant–Kovacs–Stohr (2005); Fox-problem literature.

These sources justify the machinery, not (RL_s) itself.
