# Paper 5 — Top central layer audit (2026-10-06)

## Verdict

**FAIL / CLOSED as submitted.** The replacement target
\[
C_k=D_{p^k}(W_{p^k})
\]

\[
C_k=\langle x^{p^k},y^{p^k},z^{p^k}\rangle\cong\mathbf F_p^3
\]
is not proved. The claimed one-page Jennings argument contains a false containment and a false independence inference.

## 1. What is CLOSED

For
\[
W_n=F/(RD_{n+1}),
\]
functoriality of the Zassenhaus filtration gives
\[
D_m(W_n)=D_m(F)R/(RD_{n+1})\qquad(m\le n).
\]
Hence
\[
K_k=RD_{p^{k-1}+1}/RD_{p^k+1}
=D_{p^{k-1}+1}(W_{p^k})
\]
is characteristic. Therefore the quotient map
\[
\pi_k:\operatorname{Aut}(W_{p^k})\to
\operatorname{Aut}(W_{p^{k-1}})
\]
is well-defined (not automatically surjective).

Likewise
\[
C_k=D_{p^k}(W_{p^k})
\]
is characteristic, and
\[
[C_k,W_{p^k}]\subseteq D_{p^k+1}(W_{p^k})=1,
\]
so (C_k\le Z(W_{p^k})).

These are genuine CLOSED statements.

## 2. The claimed 3-dimensional lower bound is not obtained from the stated phi witnesses

For the explicit
\[
R=\langle[x,z],[y,z],[x,y]^{-1}x^pz^{-p}\rangle^F,
\]
any homomorphism (F\to\mathbf Z_p) killing (R) must satisfy (x=z) in the abelian target, because the third relation maps to (p(x-z)=0). Thus the displayed witness
\[
x,z\mapsto t,quad y\mapsto1
\]
detects the single direction (x^{p^k}=z^{p^k}) in the abelian quotient. A second abelian witness can detect (y^{p^k}), but these give at most two independent directions by this method. The statement “(x^{p^k},y^{p^k},z^{p^k}) are all independently nonzero by phi” is therefore invalid.

Nonzero individual elements do not imply linear independence.

## 3. The key containment (R\supseteq\gamma_2(F)^p\gamma_p(F)) is not available

The relation subgroup has generators
\[
[x,z],\quad[y,z],\quad[x,y]^{-1}x^pz^{-p}.
\]
It does **not** follow that (\gamma_2(F)\subseteq R), and in particular the third generator does not give ([x,y]\in R) separately. Earlier project audits explicitly rejected the stronger premise (F^p,\gamma_{p+1}\subseteq R).

Therefore the proposed induction
\[
R\supseteq\gamma_2(F)^p\gamma_p(F)
\Longrightarrow R\supseteq\gamma_{p^k}(F)
\]
cannot be used.

More fundamentally, the non-homogeneous relation
\[
[x,y]^{-1}x^pz^{-p}
\]
has initial degree 2 but also carries higher filtered information. Passing only to the restricted ideal generated by its degree-2 initial form can lose exactly the higher-degree extension information that must be computed. Thus “(k=1) kills the commutator part, so all (k) do” is not a valid filtered induction.

## 4. The displayed decomposition of the free restricted Lie degree is not the required calculation

The assertion
\[
D_{p^k}(F)/D_{p^k+1}(F)
=\langle x^{p^k},y^{p^k},z^{p^k}\rangle
\oplus L_{p^k}
\]
with (L_{p^k}) the ordinary Lie-weight-(p^k) part is not, by itself, the quotient calculation needed here. The degree-(p^k) restricted piece also receives restricted (p)-powers of lower-degree components. The correct object is the full restricted graded component and then its quotient by the **initial/filtered relation ideal of (R)**.

Consequently the desired dimension 3 requires an actual calculation of
\[
\operatorname{gr}_{p^k}(R)
\subseteq
D_{p^k}(F)/D_{p^k+1}(F),
\]
not an induction from the (k=1) boundary.

## 5. The +9 IA conclusion is also not established

Even if (C_k\cong\mathbf F_p^3), centrality alone does not give
\[
0\to\operatorname{Hom}(V,C_k)
\to IA(W_{p^k})
\to IA(W_{p^k-1})
\to1.
\]
For automorphisms acting trivially on the quotient, the natural map into (\operatorname{Hom}(V,C_k)) is generally an **injection**, while its image is constrained by preservation of the extension class. Surjectivity requires an additional lifting/extension-class argument. Likewise surjectivity onto (IA(W_{p^k-1})) is not automatic.

Thus even (C_k\cong\mathbf F_p^3) would not by itself prove “+9 per layer”; it would at most provide a 9-dimensional ambient Hom-space for the relevant kernel.

## 6. G-stability is still OPEN

Characteristicity gives
\[
G_{p^k}\subseteq G_{p^{k-1}}.
\]
It does not give the reverse inclusion.

The earlier fact that (R\bmod D_{p+1}) is (G_p)-invariant is insufficient to conclude
\[
g(R)=R
\]
inside the full free group for every lift (g\in G_p). Nor does preservation of (D_{p^k+1}) repair this missing full-(R) invariance.

Therefore the proposed explicit-lift argument for
\[
G_{p^k}=G_p
\]
remains OPEN / LOAD-BEARING until exact invariance of the full relation subgroup (or an equivalent quotient-level extension argument) is proved.
