# PAPER 4 — JOINT RESTRICTED-POWER / EXTENSION-DEFECT GATE — 2026-10-02

## Verdict

The proposed joint carrier (P_q,B_q) splits into two mathematically different claims.

1. The restricted-power condition used to recognize the origin sector is **not a new graph-sensitive carrier**. On the specially oriented RAAG class it is forced by the abelianization torsion/free decomposition and therefore collapses to the already established q-blind Bockstein/annihilator recognition mechanism.
2. The extension-defect component B_q remains genuinely graph-sensitive locally (RP-5), but its general arbitrary-graph incidence reconstruction remains OPEN.

Thus the joint carrier is not a new closed solution. Its legitimate surviving role is: **use the already intrinsic origin-sector selector, then condition the filtered extension defect on that sector**.

## 1. Candidate

Let L_1=D_1/D_2 and L_q=D_q/D_{q+1}. Let P_q be the iterated restricted p-power map and let Delta_q be the degree-q extension-defect image. The proposed selector was

C_q = Span_Fp{x in L_1 : P_q(x) in Delta_q}.

The span is required because P_q is not linear on a non-abelian origin subspace.

## 2. Complete 3-vertex test

For the complete one-sink model, P_q is linear and the previously proved independence of s^[q],a^[q],b^[q] gives

C_q = F_p s

when Delta_q=F_p s^[q].

So the earlier arbitrary-linear-combination counterexample to J_q does not refute C_q.

## 3. Common-sink nonlinearity test

For G=<x,y,z | xyx^{-1}=y^{1+q}, xzx^{-1}=z^{1+q}>, the raw preimage P_q^{-1}(Delta_q) need not equal the origin plane because of Jacobson cross terms. However

Span{u:P_q(u) in Delta_q}=span{y,z}

is proved locally by quotienting to <x> ~= Z_p. Therefore the span correction survives this non-abelian local model.

## 4. Structural reduction: the selector is abelianization-relative

For every specially oriented RAAG in the declared convention, a special edge (v,w) has ordinary origin v and special terminus w, with wvw^{-1}=v^{1+q}. Hence the special-edge origins are precisely the degree-one directions whose abelianization has p^f-torsion at the relevant q-level. The special/terminus directions remain free.

Every extension-defect class lies in D_q/D_{q+1}, hence is invisible under the map to abelianization. If P_q(u) lies in the degree-q defect image, its abelianization must vanish. In the adjacent q-window, this forces the q-th power of the abelianization of u to vanish. Therefore u lies in the q-torsion/origin sector.

Conversely, for every special-edge origin v, P_q(v)=v^[q] lies in the degree-q defect image generated by the corresponding special commutator defect. Hence the origin sector is contained in the span-preimage.

Thus, on the specially oriented RAAG class,

C_q = O,

where O is the origin/torsion sector already recognized by the q-blind adjacent-window Bockstein carrier.

This proves that the P_q part does not provide an independent new origin-recognition theorem. It is a filtered realization/cross-check of the same abelianization-relative sector.

## 5. Consequence for novelty

The joint pair (P_q,B_q) should NOT be advertised as a new carrier merely because P_q participates in the definition. The origin selector P_q^{-1}(Delta_q) is target-relative and collapses to the existing RP-3 selector at the level of recognized sector.

What remains potentially new is the second-stage object:

B_q restricted to the intrinsically recognized origin sector, together with the finite-window mechanism that defines its degree-q defect without assuming W_q is abelian.

## 6. RP-5 survives this reduction

For the four-vertex pair

A: (a,s),(b,s)
B: (a,s),(b,t),

the common origin sector is O=span(a,b) and the special sector is S=span(s,t). The cross-defect matrices are

M_A = [[1,0],[1,0]],
M_B = [[1,0],[0,1]],

so the rank difference remains a valid same-abelianization separation witness.

Therefore the graph-sensitive part is genuinely carried by the extension defect, not by the restricted-power selector.

## 7. The real remaining theorem

The authorized load-bearing theorem is now:

**Origin-conditioned filtered incidence theorem.**

For a general specially oriented pro-p RAAG, construct from a q-blind finite Zassenhaus window an intrinsic object D_q(O,S) which:
- is defined without presentation labels or a chosen splitting;
- is invariant under filtered isomorphism;
- is insensitive to ordinary edges;
- agrees with the degree-q special-edge defect on special origin/terminus directions;
- separates non-isomorphic directed incidence patterns when the abelianization is identical.

The non-abelian obstruction is that W_q need not be abelian, so a global bilinear kappa_q on W_q is unavailable. Exact-depth centralizer jumps solve ordinary contamination locally, but arbitrary linear combinations can create accidental q-activity. The next object must therefore combine:
(1) the already recognized origin sector O,
(2) exact-depth filtering,
(3) target-labelled defect information,
without reverting to the failed Grassmannian support/intersection construction.

## 8. No-go result for the present branch

The statement

'P_q + B_q jointly solves origin recognition'

is now CLOSED as a novel claim.

The stronger statement

'P_q + B_q solves full directed-incidence reconstruction'

is NOT proved and remains OPEN.

The correct research reduction is therefore:

P_q part = recognition/cross-check, not new carrier;
B_q part = graph-sensitive candidate, still load-bearing.
