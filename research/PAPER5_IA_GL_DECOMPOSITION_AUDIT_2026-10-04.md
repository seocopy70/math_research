## Authorized executable check

A new GAP script was added:

`research/scripts/paper5_ia_gl_decomposition.g`

It checks, for all four $p=3,n=4$ cases:

1. $V=W/\Phi(W)$ and $\dim_{\mathbf F_3}V$;
2. $|GL(V)|$;
3. the induced linear image generated by `A.glAutos`;
4. that the `A.agAutos` generators act trivially on $V$;
5. $|IA(W)|$ from the `agOrder` data;
6. $v_3(|IA|)$, $v_3(|L|)$, and $v_3(|Aut(W)|)$;
7. $|IA||L|=|Aut(W)|$;
8. agreement between the directly computed linear-image order and `A.glOrder`.

The use of `glAutos` is tied to the documented AutPGrp hybrid representation: `glAutos` are the automorphisms acting nontrivially on the Frattini quotient, while `agAutos` generate the normal solvable part. This makes the decomposition script a direct test of the intended IA/GL split rather than a new ad hoc convention. See the AutPGrp documentation.
