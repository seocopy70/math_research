## 0. Decision

F2 remains operationally sealed. The next principal line is finite-window recognition, not another F2 control search.

The proposed fixed-scope chain
\[
W_{10}\to L(\rho_2)\to\{\delta_{3,\rho_3}\}\to\chi\bmod27
\]
is mathematically coherent at the already audited rank-four, p=3, k=3 scope. However, the pre-check shows an important novelty boundary: the same finite Kummer selector mechanism, in its general form

Q_k=G/P_{3^{k-1}+1}\to\mathsf K_k\to\chi_G\bmod3^k,

## 1. Mandatory pre-check

### Object
PASS. The declared finite object is the marked filtered window
\[
W_{10}(G)=(G/P_{10},P_1/P_{10},\ldots,P_9/P_{10}).
\]
The already audited Gate-A construction produces the finite lift set L(rho_2), and Gate B produces the finite connecting-map family.

### Input
PASS, with an explicit boundary. The selector construction uses only W_10 together with rho_2 recovered functorially from its W_4 truncation. It does not take q, a presentation, relator coordinates, or chi mod 27 as input. Thus rho_2 is derived input, not an external orientation input.

### Functoriality
PASS at the fixed k=3 level. Filtered isomorphisms of W_10 transport Q_10, the mod-9 carrier, the lift set, coefficient extensions, and connecting maps. Gate B's finite quotient cohomology comparison is natural.

### Gauge
PASS. No presentation generator, relator representative, free-group lift, or normalization is part of the finite selector. The earlier literal delta-family ∩ Q_4^* formulation is rejected as a type mismatch and is not used here.

### Orientation bridge
PASS at the fixed (p,k)=(3,3) mathematical level, but NOT new. The intrinsic finite selector is “the unique lift whose connecting homomorphism is zero,” and the audited variation/cup-product argument identifies that lift with chi mod 27. Existence of the canonical branch is classical Kummerian/Demushkin input; uniqueness among arbitrary candidates is the substantive finite-selector argument. This exact mechanism is already assembled for general k in Paper 2.

### q-blindness
PASS at definition level. q is absent from W_10, L(rho_2), and the connecting-map selector. This is q-blindness of the input, not a claim of q-uniform novelty.

### Separation
OPEN / LOAD-BEARING for Paper 3. The W_10 selector is a factorization/reconstruction statement for one object. By itself it does not define a category-level recognition threshold r_T(C;D_bullet). A genuine Paper 3 recognition theorem needs a pair/category statement: same W_n but different T for a lower bound, or same W_n forcing the same T for an upper bound. The F1/D versus cyclotomic pair already supplies a separate pairwise recognition example with threshold q+1, but a reusable carrier theorem has not yet been established from the W_10 chain.

### Novelty
OPEN / CONDITIONAL, with a hard negative boundary. The general bare finite quotient + arbitrary-candidate Kummer selector + automatic factorization theorem is already present in the completed Paper 2 assembly and its novelty remains conditional there. Therefore repeating it in Paper 3 is not authorized as a new contribution. The new contribution must be the recognition-threshold layer, a category enlargement, a new target, or a genuinely stronger intrinsic carrier theorem not reducible to Paper 2.

### Stop
The pre-check does NOT authorize a new W_11/W_12 computation or another Fox calculation. Such a computation would be a repetition unless it serves a separately defined recognition/separation theorem.
