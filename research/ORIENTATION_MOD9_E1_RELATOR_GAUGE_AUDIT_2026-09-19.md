# E1 RELATOR-GAUGE AUDIT FOR THE DEGREE-3 RELATION JET — 2026-09-19

## Objective

Critically test whether the candidate projective degree-3 relation jet
\[
J_3=\langle(R,P)\rangle\subset L_2\oplus L_3^{res}
\]
survives the basic freedom of changing a defining relator by conjugation.

No finite scan was used or authorized.

## 1. Conjugation gauge

Let a filtered relator have initial terms
\[
r=(R,P)+O(4),
\]
with \(R\) in degree 2 and \(P\) in restricted degree 3. Replacing the relator by
\[
r'=u r u^{-1}
\]
with degree-one initial term \(v\in V\) leaves the normal closure, hence the presented group, unchanged.

At degree 2, \(R\) is unchanged. At degree 3 the ordinary Lie contribution changes by
\[
P\longmapsto P+[v,R]
\]
up to the fixed commutator convention/sign. Thus common scaling is not the only relator gauge.

## 2. Consequence for the naive projective jet

Therefore the earlier candidate
\[
\langle(R,P)\rangle
\]
is not yet proved to be presentation-independent merely by quotienting common scaling.

The structurally safer E1 candidate must at least quotient the degree-three component by the conjugation gauge generated by
\[
[V,R].
\]

So a candidate gauge class is
\[
\bigl[R,\overline P\bigr],
\qquad
\overline P\in L_3^{res}/[V,R],
\]
with common unit/scalar ambiguity retained projectively.

This is a candidate definition, not yet a full intrinsicness theorem.

## 3. Crucial check: does the recovery functional descend?

For \(f\in V^*=H^1(G,\mathbf F_3)\), its degree-one extension annihilates ordinary Lie brackets. Hence
\[
f([v,R])=0.
\]

Therefore
\[
f(P+[v,R])=f(P).
\]

The quadratic term \((\lambda\wedge f)(R)\) is unchanged because conjugation does not change \(R\). Consequently
\[
\Theta_{R,P}(\lambda)(f)
=
f(P)+(\lambda\wedge f)(R)
\]
is unchanged under the relator-conjugation gauge.

This is the key positive result: the recovery obstruction is insensitive to the first nontrivial relator-conjugation ambiguity.

## 4. What is and is not proved

### PASS — local gauge compatibility

The \(\Theta\)-zero set survives:
- common nonzero rescaling of the relation jet;
- relator conjugation \(P\mapsto P+[V,R]\).

Thus the conjugation objection does not destroy the recovery mechanism.

### OPEN — full presentation independence

This does not prove that
\[
[R,\overline P]
\]
is canonically attached to the abstract group or to the allowed enriched filtered datum.

Still-open transformations include:
- changing the minimal free presentation/basis;
- changing the free lift by more general kernel operations;
- identifying the precise relation-module quotient that packages all legitimate relator changes.

In particular, \([V,R]\) is a necessary gauge quotient, not asserted to be the complete gauge group.

## 5. Corrected E1 status

The former statement that the projective relation jet is already intrinsic is too strong.

Correct status:
\[
\boxed{\text{E1-local gauge compatibility: PASS}}
\]
but
\[
\boxed{\text{E1-full intrinsic definition: OPEN}}.
\]

The next task is to identify a canonical relation-module object whose degree-(2,3) truncation realizes the quotient by all legitimate relator changes.

## 6. Consequence for the branch

The projective relation jet remains a viable sufficient carrier, but only after replacing a naive representative by its appropriate relation-gauge class.

No claim of mathematical minimality is made.
