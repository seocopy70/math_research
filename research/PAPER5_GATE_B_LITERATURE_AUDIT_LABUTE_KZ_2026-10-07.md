# Paper 5 Gate B literature check — Labute / Kochloukova–Zalesskii

Date: 2026-10-07

## Question
Whether the cited Demushkin literature directly supplies the missing Gate-B statement: existence of a pro-p automorphism realizing the prescribed diagonal linear part (in particular diag(a,1,a)), or an exact conjugacy/lift theorem sufficient to close the present construction.

## Labute (1967)
Labute, *Classification of Demushkin Groups*, Canad. J. Math. 19 (1967), 106–132, proves the classification of Demushkin groups and gives canonical forms for the defining relation. The paper's Theorem 2 states that, for two Demushkin relators with the same relevant invariants, there is an automorphism of the free pro-p group carrying one relator to the other. The paper also develops the transgression/cohomological machinery and filtered commutator calculations.

This is **relevant methodology**, but it does not directly state the Gate-B realization needed here: namely, for the fixed present relator and each prescribed scalar a, construct an automorphism of the quotient whose induced H^1/Frattini action is diag(a,1,a). In particular, Theorem 2 is an existence theorem for a free-group change of relator between suitable Demushkin presentations, not a ready-made prescribed-linear-part stabilizer theorem for the present Gate-B relation.

Classification: **PASS / LOCAL as literature methodology; OPEN for Gate-B applicability.**

## Kochloukova–Zalesskii, Free-by-Demushkin pro-p groups (2005)
The paper studies extensions with a Demushkin quotient and a free pro-p kernel, proving structural/cohomological results for that class. The accessible abstract and indexed text do not identify a theorem giving the required prescribed automorphism or exact conjugacy statement for the present relator. The paper therefore remains a possible structural clue, not evidence closing Gate B.

Classification: **HISTORICAL / REFERENCE ONLY for the current gate; no direct Gate-B theorem identified.**

## Amalgam / root-extension idea
The proposed expression of the present group as an amalgam/root extension is still an internal conjectural reduction. No literature result examined here establishes the exact reduction in the form needed for the Gate-B prescribed automorphism problem. It remains **OPEN**, and must not be used as a proof step.

## Consequence
The literature check does not close Gate B and does not justify replacing the current corrected finite-level computation by a citation. The best reusable result is Labute's relator-equivalence theorem as a possible route to a structural realization argument, but an explicit bridge from the prescribed linear map diag(a,1,a) to the hypotheses of that theorem is still required.
