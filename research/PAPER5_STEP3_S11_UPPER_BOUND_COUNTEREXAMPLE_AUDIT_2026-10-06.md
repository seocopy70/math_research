## Direct finite-window calculation

In (W_p), with
\[
[x,z]=[y,z]=1,\qquad [x,y]=x^p z^{-p},
\]
take any (m,a\in\mathbf F_p^\times) and define
\[
g_{m,a}:\quad x\mapsto x^m z^{a-m},\qquad y\mapsto y,\qquad z\mapsto z^a.
\]

Since (z) and (D_p(W_p)) are central of exponent (p),
\[
[g_{m,a}(x),g_{m,a}(y)]
=[x^m z^{a-m},y]
=[x,y]^m
=x^{pm}z^{-pm},
\]
while
\[
g_{m,a}(x)^p g_{m,a}(z)^{-p}
=x^{pm}z^{p(a-m)}z^{-pa}
=x^{pm}z^{-pm}.
\]
Thus the defining relation is preserved directly inside (W_p). The commutator relations with (z) are automatic. The induced map on
\[
V=W_p/D_2(W_p)
\]
has diagonal entries (m,1,a), hence is invertible; therefore (g_{m,a}) is an automorphism.

Consequently the parameters (m) and (a) are independent. In particular, choosing (m\ne a) gives an actual automorphism whose (V)-matrix is not in the previously proposed
\[
S_{11}(p)=\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}a&b&0\\0&1&0\\0&d&a\end{pmatrix}
\right\}.
\]

The torsion-subgroup argument does **not** force (m=a). In
\[
A=W_p^{ab}=\mathbf Z_p^3/\langle p(e_x-e_z)\rangle,
\]
the torsion subgroup is generated by (t=e_x-e_z). For
\[
g(x)=x^m z^c,\qquad g(z)=z^a,
\]
torsion preservation gives
\[
g_*(t)=m e_x+(c-a)e_z\in\langle e_x-e_z\rangle,
\]
hence
\[
c=a-m.
\]
But this is exactly the relation already obtained from preservation of
\([x,y]=x^p z^{-p}); it does **not** imply (m=a).
