# ORIENTATION MOD-9 — CARRIER CATEGORY / COARSEST QUOTIENT AUDIT — 2026-09-19

## Objective

Resolve the remaining question whether a mathematically meaningful carrier category can be defined before asking for absolute minimality of the projective degree-(2,3) relation jet.

The conclusion is a **qualified PASS**:

- a natural category of degree-(2,3) relation carriers can be defined independently of the recovered orientation value;
- within the quotient class generated by the degree-(2,3) jet and preserving **all degree-one evaluation observables** used by \(\Theta\), there is a canonical coarsest quotient;
- the raw projective jet \(J_3\) is **not** minimal in that category;
- the coarsest quotient retains the quadratic alternating form \(R\) and only the degree-3 component visible in degree-one evaluation, namely the restricted-cube/abelianized component \(p(P)\);
- absolute minimality among arbitrary alternative carriers remains not well-posed.

This is a stronger and more useful result than simply declaring \(J_3\) minimal.

## 1. Degree-(2,3) carrier category

Fix a 4-dimensional \(\mathbf F_3\)-vector space \(V\).

An admissible raw carrier is a pair
\[
(R,P),\qquad R\in\Lambda^2V\setminus\{0\},\quad
P\in L^{res}_3(V),
\]
with \(R\) nondegenerate in the Demushkin case.

The allowed gauge is
\[
(R,P)\sim(uR,uP+[v,R]),
\]
where \(u\in\mathbf F_3^\times\) (or the corresponding unit before reduction) and \(v\in V\).

A quotient carrier is required to be obtained functorially from this data and to preserve every observable
\[
\Theta_{R,P}(\lambda)(f)
=f(P)+(\lambda\wedge f)(R),
\qquad \lambda,f\in V^*.
\]

The category is deliberately defined from the filtered degree-(2,3) structure and its natural degree-one evaluations, not by declaring the desired orientation value itself to be the carrier.

## 2. The degree-3 information seen by \(\Theta\)

There is a canonical quotient
\[
L^{res}_3(V)\longrightarrow
L^{res}_3(V)/[V,L_2(V)].
\]

In degree 3, the ordinary Lie-bracket part is contained in \([V,L_2(V)]\). What survives is the restricted-cube part
\[
V^{(1)}=\langle X^{[3]}:X\in V\rangle.
\]

Over \(\mathbf F_3\), the Frobenius twist may be identified with \(V\), but keeping \(V^{(1)}\) in the statement makes the construction coordinate-free.

Write
\[
p(P)\in V^{(1)}
\]
for the image of \(P\).

For every degree-one functional \(f\),
\[
f(P)=f(p(P)).
\]

Also
\[
f([v,R])=0.
\]

Therefore \(\Theta\) factors through
\[
S(R,P):=(R,p(P)).
\]

Thus the raw degree-3 Lie component contains strictly more information than the recovery functional can see.

## 3. Canonical compressed carrier

Define the projective compressed carrier
\[
\boxed{
\overline J_3=[(R,p)]
\subset \mathbf P\bigl(\Lambda^2V\oplus V^{(1)}\bigr)
}
\]
with the same common-unit scaling.

Its recovery functional is
\[
\overline\Theta_{(R,p)}(\lambda)(f)
=f(p)+(\lambda\wedge f)(R).
\]

The gauge \(P\mapsto P+[v,R]\) disappears automatically.

For the frozen q=3 and q=\infty examples:
\[
\overline J_3(3)=[(R_2,e_1^{(1)})],
\qquad
\overline J_3(\infty)=[(R_2,0)].
\]

Hence
\[
Z(\overline\Theta_{3})=\{e_2^*\},
\qquad
Z(\overline\Theta_{\infty})=\{0\}.
\]

The q-separation and mod-9 recovery are unchanged.

## 4. Coarsest-quotient theorem

Let \(q:J_3\to C\) be any functorial quotient carrier in the above category such that every degree-one observable \(\Theta_J(\lambda)(f)\) factors through \(C\).

Then equality in \(C\) must imply equality of:

1. the projective quadratic relation line \([R]\), and
2. the degree-3 evaluation class \(p(P)\) relative to that line.

Indeed, if two jets have the same image in \(C\) but different \(p(P)\), choose \(f\in V^*\) with
\[
f(p(P)-p(P'))\ne0.
\]
Then the values of \(\Theta\) differ already at \(\lambda=0\), contradicting factorization.

Likewise, if their quadratic forms differ in a way not removed by the allowed projective gauge, nondegeneracy of the pairing
\[
(\lambda,f)\mapsto(\lambda\wedge f)(R)
\]
provides degree-one observables distinguishing them.

Consequently every such quotient factors uniquely through \(\overline J_3\).

Equivalently, in the category whose objects are functorial quotients of \(J_3\) preserving all degree-one \(\Theta\)-observables and whose morphisms are factor maps,
\[
\boxed{
\overline J_3\text{ is terminal (the coarsest admissible quotient).}
}
\]

This is the correct categorical direction: “minimal information” corresponds to terminality among quotient carriers, not initiality.

## 5. What this proves about the original \(J_3\)

The raw projective jet \(J_3\) is therefore **not absolutely minimal even inside the natural degree-(2,3) quotient category**.

The genuinely retained information is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{quadratic symplectic relation line }[R]
\quad+
\quad
\text{restricted-cubic vector }[p(P)]\text{ relative to }[R].
}
\]

For the q=3 normal form the extra datum is represented by
\[
p(P_3)=e_1^{(1)}.
\]

The ordinary degree-3 bracket components and the \([V,L_2]\) gauge do not contribute to the recovery observable.

## 6. Why this is not a tautological “store the answer” construction

The compressed carrier does not store \(\chi\), \(\lambda_\chi\), or q.

It is obtained by a canonical algebraic quotient of the filtered degree-3 relation layer:
\[
L^{res}_3(V)
\twoheadrightarrow
L^{res}_3(V)/[V,L_2(V)]
\cong V^{(1)}.
\]

Only after forming this carrier do we solve the intrinsic equation
\[
f(p)+(\lambda\wedge f)(R)=0
\quad\forall f.
\]

Thus the compression is independent of the chosen recovered orientation.

## 7. Remaining boundary: recovery-only minimality

If the requirement is weakened from preserving **all** \(\Theta\)-values to preserving only the final zero set
\[
Z(\Theta),
\]
then a still smaller carrier may exist: one can compress the pair \(([R],p)\) all the way to the unique covector determined by
\[
\Phi_R(\lambda)(f)=(\lambda\wedge f)(R)=-f(p).
\]

But that object is already essentially the recovered orientation covector itself. Calling it an independent “relation carrier” would make minimality tautological.

Therefore the meaningful non-tautological endpoint is the coarsest quotient preserving the full natural evaluation family, namely \(\overline J_3\).

## 8. Final decision

### PASS

A defensible carrier category exists, and it yields a genuine universal property:

\[
\boxed{
J_3\twoheadrightarrow\overline J_3
}
\]

where \(\overline J_3\) is the terminal/coarsest quotient among degree-(2,3) filtered relation carriers preserving all natural degree-one recovery observables.

### FAIL / CLOSED

The stronger claim

\[
J_3\text{ itself is absolutely minimal}
\]

is false in this natural quotient category.

### OPEN / not well-posed without extra axioms

Minimality among **all possible** intrinsic carriers, including constructions not obtained as quotients of the degree-(2,3) relation jet, is still not a mathematically determined question. It requires an independently justified larger category.

## 9. Research consequence

The categorical branch should now stop trying to prove minimality of the raw pair \((R,P)\).

The structurally meaningful object is the compressed carrier
\[
\boxed{
\overline J_3=[(R,p(P))].
}
\]

The next legitimate question, if desired, is whether this compressed carrier admits an analogous exact \(\mathbf Z_3\)-coefficient formulation and whether its q=3 exact version recovers the full orientation without retaining irrelevant degree-3 Lie data.
