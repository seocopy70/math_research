# 01. Mathematical Motivation

## Why this research begins with a Demuškin group

The research question is not merely to classify a finite-dimensional representation. The representation-theoretic calculations are probes of a deeper arithmetic question:

> Can the canonical orientation character of a rank-4 pro-3 Demuškin group be recovered from intrinsic filtered/graded data?

We study

\[
G=\langle x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4\mid x_1^3[x_1,x_2][x_3,x_4]=1\rangle,
\]

with commutator convention

\[
[a,b]=a^{-1}b^{-1}ab.
\]

A Demuškin group carries a canonical orientation character

\[
\chi:G\to \mathbb Z_3^\times.
\]

The important point is that this character is 3-adic information. The target has the structure

\[
\mathbb Z_3^\times\simeq \mu_2\times(1+3\mathbb Z_3).
\]

Passing to mod-3 information can therefore forget part of the orientation character. This creates the central tension of the project.

## The filtration question

The Zassenhaus/Jennings-Lazard filtration gives intrinsic layers

\[
D_1\supset D_2\supset D_3\supset\cdots
\]

and an associated graded restricted Lie object

\[
\operatorname{gr}(G;\mathbb F_3).
\]

The research asks how much of \(\chi\) is encoded in this internal structure.

There are three logically different possibilities:

1. the graded/filtered structure canonically determines \(\chi\);
2. it determines only a reduction or partial shadow of \(\chi\);
3. it is insufficient, and an additional higher or 3-adic datum is necessary.

The project does not assume in advance which of these is true.

## Why the quadratic relation matters

The initial quadratic relation is

\[
R=[X_1,X_2]+[X_3,X_4].
\]

It has the form of a symplectic pairing. This naturally introduces

\[
Sp_4(\mathbb F_3)
\]

as the symmetry group of the degree-1 data preserving the quadratic relation.

The degree-4 element

\[
T=[[[X_3,X_4],X_1],X_1]
\]

is then used as a concrete probe of what the relation and its symmetries produce at higher degree.

T is **not** assumed to be canonical. Indeed Phase 1 proves that it is not invariant under the natural symplectic action.

Thus the research moves from the individual vector T to the full orbit module generated by T.

## Why Phase 2 exists

The orbit span

\[
W=\langle Sp_4(\mathbb F_3)\cdot T\rangle
\]

has dimension 45. The purpose of Phase 2 is to determine whether this larger, symmetry-respecting structure contains canonical subquotients or extension data that could be relevant to orientation.

The current results are:

\[
W\cong\Lambda^2(\operatorname{Sym}^2V),
\]

and there is a filtration

\[
0\subset U_{10}\subset K_{35}\subset W_{45},
\]

with

\[
U\cong\operatorname{Sym}^2(V),
\]

and a 25-dimensional irreducible quotient

\[
M=K/U.
\]

Writing

\[
E=W/U,
\]

we have the non-split exact sequence

\[
0\to M_{25}\to E_{35}\to\operatorname{Sym}^2(V)_{10}\to0.
\]

The point is not that E has already been identified with a known representation. The point is that the filtered structure generated by the original degree-4 probe is highly constrained and non-semisimple.

## Why this could matter for orientation

The representation theory is therefore a diagnostic layer between the raw filtration and the final orientation question:

\[
\text{Demuškin relation}
\to
\text{graded obstruction}
\to
\text{symmetry orbit}
\to
\text{canonical subquotients/extensions}
\to
\text{possible orientation data}.
\]

This chain is a research strategy, not a theorem. In particular, none of the Phase 2 results currently proves that \(\chi\) can be reconstructed.

## What would count as a final result?

A positive result would require an explicitly defined construction, intrinsic to the filtered group, producing a character

\[
\chi_{\mathrm{filt}}:G\to\mathbb Z_3^\times
\]

and a proof that it agrees with the canonical Demuškin orientation.

A negative result could instead prove that the available filtered/graded data admit two non-isomorphic orientations, or otherwise cannot distinguish the relevant 3-adic information. In that case the research should identify the smallest additional datum needed for reconstruction.

## Status

**OPEN.** The final orientation-reconstruction problem is not solved.
