## 1. Position

O2-4 showed that the absolute obstruction image O_tau = Im D_tau depends on the admissible transport.

O2-5 showed that the affine variation Delta D = D_1-D_0 has rank 10 and H-stable image.

O2-6 asks whether this variation direction itself depends on which admissible transport is chosen as the basepoint.

## 2. Experiment

The frozen B1 admissible family is tau_b = tau(I+bN), b in F_3.

The three possible finite differences were compared as actual 4096x45 maps:
D_1-D_0, D_2-D_1, D_0-D_2.

This is stronger than comparing ranks or image dimensions.

## 3. Verified result

Run 35284130822 completed successfully.

The log reports:
- rank(D_1-D_0) = 10
- rank(D_2-D_1) = 10
- rank(D_0-D_2) = 10
- D_1-D_0 = D_2-D_1: True
- D_2-D_1 = D_0-D_2: True
- D_0-D_2 = D_1-D_0: True

Therefore the actual variation map, not merely its image, is independent of the choice of basepoint within the complete frozen B1 family.

D_1-D_0 = D_2-D_1 = D_0-D_2 = Delta D, with rank Delta D = 10.

Consequently Delta O = Im Delta D is independent of the basepoint within this admissible family.

## 4. Logical interpretation

This closes the specific basepoint-dependence loophole left by O2-5.

Because the B1 admissible normalized transports are exactly the three points tau_0, tau_1, tau_2 of the verified affine family, there is no remaining basepoint choice inside that family that changes the variation direction.

The correct statement is:

> The absolute obstruction image is transport-dependent, but the affine variation direction of the obstruction is canonical within the full verified B1 admissible affine family.

This is stronger than the O2-5 statement, but it is still deliberately narrower than claiming that Delta O has been characterized purely from filtration data without reference to the B1 transport-solution space.

## 5. What is now established

- O2-4: absolute obstruction O_tau is not canonical.
- O2-5: its affine variation has a 10-dimensional H-stable image.
- O2-6: that variation direction is independent of the basepoint across the complete verified B1 family.

Thus the transport freedom does not destroy the variation object.

## 6. Remaining boundary

This does not yet prove:
- a transport-free formula for Delta O stated solely in terms of the filtered/graded data;
- a canonical identification Delta O ≅ U;
- distinction of q=3 from q=infinity;
- recovery of chi.

The next stage may therefore move to the explicit q=3 versus q=infinity comparison, while carrying Delta O as a now-controlled candidate invariant.
