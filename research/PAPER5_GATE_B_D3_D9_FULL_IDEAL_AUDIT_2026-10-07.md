## Why the homogeneous-component construction is not automatically too weak

The B4 script constructs the degree-N span from every homogeneous component
\[
r_d=(r-1)_d
\]
with all left/right monomial contexts of total remaining degree N-d.

For a fixed N, this is exactly the degree-N component of the filtered ideal generated by r-1:
\[
(I+\Delta^{N+1})/\Delta^{N+1}.
\]

The important qualification is that a single homogeneous component calculation would not by itself prove exact normal-closure membership. But the script checks the homogeneous target component degree by degree. Since the target W^3-X_2 has no components below degree 4, simultaneous membership through degrees 4,...,9 gives the corresponding truncated statement
\[
W^3-X_2\in I+\Delta^{10},
\]
not merely membership in an unrelated stronger ideal.

Thus the earlier phrase “J-membership only” was too coarse and should not be used to demote the D3-D9 finite-window result.
