# PAPER 5 — CRITICAL REVIEW OF PROPOSED ORBIT-CATEGORY / MINIMALITY ENDPOINT
## 2026-10-03

### Verdict

The proposed orbit-category construction is useful as an auxiliary action/orbit formalism, but the proposed minimality theorem and the claimed categorical endpoint are not accepted.

The current authoritative C2 boundary remains controlling:
- canonical/characteristic admissible quotient: FAIL / CLOSED;
- target-free one-component realization groupoid for the declared critical class: PASS / CLOSED;
- characteristic/coarsest compression: OPEN;
- absolute minimality: OPEN.

### 1. Orbit category is not the missing mathematical object

The admissible realization groupoid already has source-automorphism and target-isomorphism morphisms. Passing to an Aut(W_n)-orbit category by taking orbits of objects therefore risks quotienting structure that is already part of the realization groupoid.

Moreover, the proposed definition Hom = pi_0(Map_Aut(orbit, orbit')) is not justified from the existing realization-groupoid structure. No topology, simplicial enrichment, or mapping-space model has been defined for the finite realization groupoid. Consequently pi_0(Map_...) is not currently a well-defined object in the project's declared category.

A discrete-action/orbit groupoid can be defined if desired, but that is a separate construction and requires an explicit 2-categorical/groupoid convention.

### 2. Connected does not mean trivial

The statement that a connected realization groupoid/orbit category is "single object, single morphism" is false in general. A connected groupoid may have a nontrivial automorphism group. Likewise, transitivity of an action on objects does not imply triviality of the corresponding action groupoid.

Thus the proposed dichotomy "orbit category trivial => pi_0(R) is minimal" versus "orbit category nontrivial => orbit structure is minimal" does not follow.

### 3. Minimality is presently undefined

The phrase "smaller than R^ad" has no declared comparison category, morphism, or information preorder. The continuity protocol explicitly requires minimality to be relative to a fixed admissible category.

The candidate theorem therefore cannot be promoted from conjectural wording to theorem form until one specifies:
- the class of admissible characteristic/intrinsic objects;
- what counts as a factorization of the Boolean through such an object;
- what "smaller" means;
- whether equivalence is categorical equivalence, quotient/factorization, or information-theoretic refinement.

### 4. The proposed no-go proof has a genuine logical gap

Showing that the intersection of all admissible kernels is too small and the subgroup generated by all admissible kernels collapses to the abelianization only rules out those two constructions.

It does not imply that every characteristic finite invariant, or every characteristic quotient, fails. The repository already records this exact boundary.

An arbitrary characteristic object could encode the Aut(W_n)-action on the admissible realization set without being itself a quotient W_n/N obtained by intersection/generation of kernels.

Therefore the proposed "No-go for characteristic compression" must remain bounded:
- FAIL / CLOSED for the two tested universal kernel constructions;
- OPEN for all other characteristic/intrinsic compressions.

### 5. The correct useful content

Two results are worth retaining after reformulation.

(a) Auxiliary action formalism: Aut(W_n) acts on admissible quotient maps/realizations, and the established critical-window orbit theorem shows that the declared admissible class is one two-sided orbit.

(b) Orbit-invariance of the relative Boolean: the split/non-split obstruction is constant on that orbit. This is already captured by the target-free realization groupoid theorem and should not be repackaged as a new minimality theorem.

### 6. Consequence for Paper 5

Do not add MINIMALITY.md with the proposed theorem, and do not change the current status of coarsest intrinsic compression to CLOSED.

Do not treat the orbit category as a proven Paper-5 endpoint.

The surviving structural question is narrower and stronger:

Is there a precisely defined smaller intrinsic/characteristic object, in a declared admissible category, through which the connected realization groupoid and its relative Boolean factor?

A positive answer advances coarsest intrinsic realization. A negative answer requires a universal no-go over that explicitly declared category, not merely failure of two kernel operations.

### Classification

- Aut(W_n)-action on admissible realizations: PASS / LOCAL as an auxiliary formalism;
- one-orbit property: PASS / CLOSED under the established critical-window orbit theorem;
- proposed orbit-category-as-minimal-object: FAIL / CLOSED as currently formulated;
- two extreme characteristic kernel constructions: FAIL / CLOSED;
- general characteristic compression no-go: OPEN;
- coarsest intrinsic realization: OPEN;
- absolute minimality: OPEN.

### Stop

No new orbit-category computation is authorized merely to prove the proposed minimality statement. The next branch must first define the admissible comparison category/order for compression; only after that definition passes the pre-check may a genuine minimality/no-go test be run.
