## Result

There exists an \(Sp_4(\mathbb F_3)\)-module isomorphism
\[
X:W_{45}\xrightarrow{\sim}W_d
\]
such that
\[
A_i^{(d)}X=XA_i^{(45)}
\]
for every tested generator, and
\[
X|_K=\mathrm{id}_K.
\]
Therefore the two exact sequences
\[
0\to K\to W_{45}\to\operatorname{Sym}^2(V)\to0
\]
and
\[
0\to K\to W_d\to\operatorname{Sym}^2(V)\to0
\]
are equivalent relative to the common submodule \(K\).

## Structural interpretation

This is a negative result for the hypothesis that the q-sensitive element
\(d=[X_1^{[3]},X_2]\) produces a distinct extension class at this 45-dimensional
module level.

Combined with A3-4-6, the q-sensitive construction gives the same quotient
representation \(\operatorname{Sym}^2(V)\), and A3-4-9 shows that even the extension
class relative to \(K\) is equivalent to that of \(W_{45}\).

The constrained solution space has nullity 1. This is consistent with the remaining
scalar freedom after fixing \(K\) pointwise; the existence of a full-rank solution is
the substantive statement.

## What is NOT proved

This does **not** prove that q-sensitive information disappears from the entire
restricted Lie algebra or from all higher filtration structure. It also does not prove
canonical orientation.

It only proves that q-sensitive information is not distinguished by this particular
45-dimensional module together with its common 35-dimensional submodule and extension
class.

## Consequence for the next experiment

Repeating the same module/extension comparison is no longer informative. The next
stage should search for structure that survives this equivalence, for example:

1. the restricted p-map on the relevant modules;
2. higher filtration operations or brackets involving the q-sensitive class;
3. embedding data into the ambient restricted Lie algebra rather than abstract module
   structure;
4. a higher-degree intrinsic operation from which the candidate \(\lambda\pmod 3\)
   could be recovered.

The working principle is therefore:

> **A3-4-9 closes the module-extension route at this level. The q-sensitive signal,
> if it carries the desired intrinsic information, must survive in structure beyond
> the equivalence class of the 45-dimensional \(Sp_4(\mathbb F_3)\)-module extension.**

This record is a computational result, not a proof of canonical orientation.
