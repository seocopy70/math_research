## 2026-10-06 correction

The submitted IA(W_p)=F_p^2 claim is rejected. For n<p, W_n is elementary abelian of rank 3, hence Aut(W_n)=GL_3(F_p) and IA(W_n)=1. For n=p, D_p(W_p) is central elementary abelian of dimension 2; independent central shears of x,y,z by arbitrary D_p elements give IA(W_p)=F_p^6. Therefore the p^2(p-1) order claim for n<p is false. The general S_11 upper bound remains OPEN because the preceding proof used identities in F/R for arbitrary finite-window automorphisms without an independent lifting theorem.
