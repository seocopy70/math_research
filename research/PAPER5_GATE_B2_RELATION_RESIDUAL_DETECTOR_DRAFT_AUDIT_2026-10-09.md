## 2. Structural check of \(K_N\)

With
\[
I_N=\overline{\sum_{ip^j\ge N,\ i\ge1,\ j\ge0}p^jJ^i},
\qquad \operatorname{gr}_N=I_N/I_{N+1},
\]
reduction modulo \(p\) induces a surjection
\[
\pi_N:\operatorname{gr}_N\longrightarrow J^N/J^{N+1}.
\]
The kernel has the following definition-level description:
\[
\boxed{
K_N\cong
\bigoplus_{\substack{j\ge1\\p^j\mid N}}
\frac{p^jJ^{N/p^j}+I_{N+1}}{I_{N+1}}
\cong
\bigoplus_{\substack{j\ge1\\p^j\mid N}}
J^{N/p^j}/J^{N/p^j+1}
}
\]
as \(\mathbf F_p\)-vector spaces, using the degree-\(N/p^j\) coefficient component in the \(j\)-th summand. In particular, \(K_N=0\) if \(p\nmid N\). This formula should be included only after the filtration conventions and the direct-sum independence of the distinct augmentation degrees are stated explicitly.

This is a structural consequence of the proposed \(I_N\), not a relation-level detection result. It says where a mod-\(p\)-invisible class could live; it does not show that the particular residual \(R(\psi)\) has a nonzero class there.

## 3. Type and invariance issues to fix before calculation

### 3.1 \(B_N\) is not yet defined

The phrase “the variation subspace generated by coordinate/lift changes” is not sufficient. The allowed transformations must be specified as maps on the completed integral Magnus algebra or on the free pro-\(p\) group, and must preserve the chosen filtration and the admissibility condition \(R(\psi)\in I_N\).

A safe provisional definition is: fix \(N\), define the set \(\mathcal A_N\) of admissible pairs (coordinate choice, lift \(\psi\)) satisfying \(R(\psi)\in I_N\), and only after proving that every permitted change induces a well-defined map on \(\operatorname{gr}_N\) and preserves \(K_N\), define \(B_N\) as the \(\mathbf F_p\)-span of differences of the resulting classes. This is still a *definition target*, not a theorem: if the transformations do not act on \(K_N\), \(K_N/B_N\) is ill-typed and the proposed detector must be reformulated.

### 3.2 Coordinate changes

“\(x\mapsto1+X\) versus another lift” is not a precise coordinate-change rule. Specify whether a change is a continuous \(\mathbf Z_p\)-algebra automorphism of \(\mathcal A\), a change of free pro-\(p\) basis, or a change of Magnus expansion. Prove it preserves \(I_N\) and describe its induced action on \(\operatorname{gr}_N\). These are not interchangeable notions.

### 3.3 Relator representative changes

For \(w_0\mapsto u w_0^\varepsilon u^{-1}\), the residual changes nonlinearly and also depends on how \(\psi\) is transported. Specify that transport and prove the induced class changes by a well-defined element of \(B_N\). Do not assume the effect is additive before proving it.

### 3.4 \(C(N-1)\) and the first nonzero level

The condition \(C(N-1)\) is \(R(\psi)\in I_N\). The assertion that \([R]\ne0\) in \(\operatorname{gr}_N\) means \(R(\psi)\notin I_{N+1}\). The first candidate \(N\) must be determined by the filtration and admissible equations, not assumed in advance. Also verify that \(R(\psi)\) is indeed an element of the stated completed algebra under the chosen exponent and Magnus conventions.

## 4. Repair the pre-registered falsification criteria

- **K1 (vanishing):** One selected \(\psi\) with \([R]=0\) does *not* falsify a detector intended to work for some admissible lift. To falsify the stated universal candidate at the first admissible \((p,N)\), specify whether the claim is “every admissible lift has zero class” or “the canonical class modulo \(B_N\) is zero,” and test exactly that claim. If the intended claim is existence of a nonzero invariant class, failure to find one in a finite search is not by itself proof of nonexistence unless the admissible parameter space is exhaustively and correctly defined.
- **K2 (gauge removal):** This is meaningful only after \(B_N\subseteq K_N\) is proved. If the class of the residual is zero in \(K_N/B_N\), the proposed invariant obstruction is absent for that \(N\).
- **K3 (pure-power collapse):** The pure-power identity already recorded in the B-1 audit is a closed negative route. Apply K3 only after proving that the proposed noncommutative residual is actually equivalent to that pure-power residual under an allowed transformation; superficial resemblance is insufficient.
- **K4 (inconsistent cancellation):** If \(C(N-1)\) has no solution, distinguish a contradiction in the mathematical hypothesis from a contradiction caused by an over-restrictive or incorrectly parameterized \(\Psi\). Only the latter calls for revising the ansatz; neither licenses increasing \(N\) without revisiting the definition.

The “do not raise \(N\) after a failed test” rule is accepted, with the qualification that a failure caused by an identified definition/type error must first be recorded as such, not misreported as a mathematical counterexample.

## 6. Critical follow-up — group-element residual collapses by B-1 (2026-10-09)

A direct logical incompatibility was found in the revised definition, before any computation.

The proposed residual is
\[
z=\psi_{u,v}(w_0)w_0^{-\tilde\alpha}\in F,
\qquad R=\mu(z)-1.
\]
Thus it is the Magnus residual of an actual free pro-\(p\) group element. Gate B-1 has already established, for group elements,
\[
\mu(g)-1\in I_n^{\mathrm{int}}
\iff g\in D_n(F)
\iff \bar\mu(g)-1\in J^n.
\]
But the definition of \(\Psi_N\) requires both (i) \(R\in I_N\) and (ii) \(\bar R\in J^{N+1}\). Applying B-1 at level \(N+1\) to \(z\) gives \(z\in D_{N+1}(F)\), hence \(R\in I_{N+1}\). Therefore \([R]=0\) in \(I_N/I_{N+1}\), and in particular its class in \(K_N\) is zero.

Consequently, under the stated B-1 theorem and the current definition of \(R\),
\[
\Psi_N\ne\varnothing\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\{[R(\psi)]:\psi\in\Psi_N\}=\{0\}.
\]
If \(\Psi_N=\varnothing\), there is no witness either. This is a definitional no-go, not a computational result.
