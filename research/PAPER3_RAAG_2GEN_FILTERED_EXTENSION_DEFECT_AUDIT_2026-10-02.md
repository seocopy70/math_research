# PAPER 3 FOLLOW-UP — 2-GENERATOR SPECIAL-EDGE FILTERED EXTENSION DEFECT — 2026-10-02

## Decision

The proposed replacement for the ill-typed \(\Lambda^2L_1\to L_q\) construction is available directly from the finite central extension
\[
1\to A_n:=D_n/D_{n+1}\to W_{n+1}:=G/D_{n+1}\to W_n:=G/D_n\to1.
\]

For the 2-generator special-edge model
\[
G=\langle v,w\mid wvw^{-1}=v^{1+q}\rangle,\qquad q=p^f,
\]
one has
\[
G' = \overline{\langle v^q\rangle}\subseteq D_q.
\]
Hence \(W_n\) is abelian for every \(n\le q\), and at \(n=q\) the central extension
\[
1\to A_q\to W_{q+1}\to W_q\to1
\]
has a canonical commutator pairing
\[
\kappa_q:W_q\times W_q\to A_q,qquad
\kappa_q(\bar x,\bar y)=[\tilde x,\tilde y].
\]
Its image is exactly
\[
\operatorname{im}\kappa_q
=
G'D_{q+1}/D_{q+1}
=
\mathbf F_p\,\overline{v^q}.
\]

This is the correct intrinsic carrier of the first q-defect. It is not the associated-graded bracket and not \(\operatorname{gr}(R)\) by itself.

## 1. Critical correction to the proposed relation-module route

The statement "the q-correction \(v^q\) appears in \(\operatorname{gr}_q(R)\)" is too quick and should NOT be used.

For the free presentation \(F\twoheadrightarrow G\) with relator
\[
r=[w,v]v^{-q},
\]
the relator itself has initial Zassenhaus degree 2. The factor \(v^q\) is a correction term in the ambient filtered free group, but \([w,v]\notin R\), so one cannot simply subtract the degree-2 term inside the relation subgroup and call the remainder an element of \(R\).

Thus the filtered relation-module object must be realized through the finite extension/crossed commutator defect, not by the naive filtration \(R\cap D_n(F)\) alone.

## 2. Intrinsic object

For any finite window containing \(W_{n+1}\), define
\[
A_n=\ker(W_{n+1}\to W_n)
\]
and, whenever \(W_n\) is abelian, define
\[
\kappa_n:W_n\times W_n\to A_n
\]
by commutator of lifts.

Because \(D_n/D_{n+1}\) is central in \(G/D_{n+1}\), \(\kappa_n\) is lift-independent. Because \(W_n\) is abelian, it is alternating and bi-multiplicative.

No presentation, generator labels, relator, or q is used in the definition.

For the special-edge model:
- \(\operatorname{im}\kappa_n=0\) for \(n<q\);
- \(\operatorname{im}\kappa_q=\mathbf F_p\overline{v^q}\neq0\);
- therefore
\[
q=\min\{n:\operatorname{im}\kappa_n\neq0\}.
\]

This gives a q-blind intrinsic detection rule from the finite window.

## 3. First-survival and carrier proof

The identity \([w,v]=v^q\) gives \(G'=\overline{\langle v^q\rangle}\). Since \(\deg_Z(v^q)=q\),
\[
G'\subseteq D_n\quad(n\le q),
\]
so \(W_n\) is abelian for \(n\le q\).

At \(n=q\),
\[
[v,w]D_{q+1}=v^{-q}D_{q+1}\neq1,
\]
hence \(\operatorname{im}\kappa_q\neq0\). Since \(G'\) is procyclic generated by \(v^q\), its image in \(D_q/D_{q+1}\) is one-dimensional.

Classification:
**PASS / LOCAL** — intrinsic first-defect carrier and q-detection in the 2-generator model.

This upgrades the previous OPEN role-recognition gate, but only for this 2-generator model.

## 4. Sinkhole/origin line

The restricted power operation
\[
P_q:L_1=D_1/D_2\to L_q=D_q/D_{q+1}
\]
is canonical as the iterated restricted p-operation.

The carrier line
\[
C_q:=\operatorname{im}\kappa_q=\mathbf F_p\overline{v^q}
\]
therefore gives the intrinsic condition
\[
P_q(x)\in C_q.
\]

In the present rank-2 model, the projection \(G\to\langle w\rangle\cong\mathbf Z_p\) sends \(P_q(\bar v)\) to 0 and \(P_q(\bar w)\) to the nonzero class of \(w^q\). Thus the q-power image of any degree-one class with nonzero w-component has nonzero w^q-component and cannot lie in \(C_q\). Consequently
\[
\{x\in L_1:P_q(x)\in C_q\}=\mathbf F_p\bar v.
\]

This identifies the **origin/ordinary line** intrinsically. The complementary quotient
\[
L_1/\mathbf F_p\bar v
\]
is the one-dimensional **sinkhole direction**.

Important limitation: the complement is naturally a quotient line, not a canonically selected complementary subspace. Thus "the sinkhole vertex is a canonical line in \(L_1\)" is stronger than what has been proved.

Classification:
**PASS / LOCAL** for intrinsic origin-line recognition;
**CONDITIONAL** for a literal sinkhole-line statement unless an additional splitting/action construction is supplied.

## 5. Gauge audit

For
\[
\phi_{a,c}(v)=v^a,\qquad \phi_{a,c}(w)=v^cw,

\[
\phi_{a,c}(v^q)=v^{aq},
\]
so the defect line \(C_q\) is preserved as a line. The canonical orientation is also preserved under the correct convention
\[
\theta(v)=1,\qquad\theta(w)=1+q.
\]

Thus the former gauge obstruction is definitively rejected.

Classification:
- gauge obstruction: **FAIL / CLOSED**;
- gauge covariance of the intrinsic defect: **PASS / LOCAL**.

## 6. Orientation bridge

The intrinsic carrier detects the first nonzero commutator-defect degree q. In the standard special-edge family, the literature identifies the canonical orientation by
\[
\theta(v)=1,\qquad \theta(w)=1+q.
\]
Blumer–Quadrelli–Weigel, Theorem 4.9, states that a torsion-free Kummerian orientation exists exactly for specially oriented graphs and then equals the canonical orientation. The source also gives the canonical value \(1+q\) on the special/sinkhole vertex and 1 on ordinary vertices.

Therefore, **at the declared standard 2-generator family**, the finite-window carrier yields q and the known canonical orientation formula yields theta.

This is a valid orientation bridge at family/classification scope, but it is not a new direct theorem that reconstructs theta without using the known special-edge classification. It should not be promoted as a novel global orientation theorem.

Classification:
**PASS / LOCAL** for the declared family;
**NOVELTY = OPEN** and no new-bridge claim is made.

## 7. What remains genuinely open

The successful construction does NOT prove the general RAAG gate.

Open load-bearing questions:
1. For a general specially oriented RAAG, does \(\operatorname{im}\kappa_n\) intrinsically separate ordinary edges from special directed edges?
2. Can several special edges at the same degree be separated into their individual sinkhole supports?
3. Can the quotient-line mechanism be upgraded to a canonical vertex/sinkhole object without choosing a splitting?
4. Does the finite-window construction remain natural for all admissible window morphisms, not only isomorphisms?
5. Is the resulting carrier genuinely new relative to the frozen Paper 3 selector once rank/graph data are allowed?

No large computation is authorized before these structural questions are answered.
