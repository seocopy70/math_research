# Paper 5 — Next-step audit: corrected relation-jet route
Date: 2026-10-05

## Trigger
A new independent review identified a mismatch between the abstract model
\(\Lambda^2V\oplus V^{(1)}\) and the previously stated \(S_{11}\) formula, and proposed a corrected restricted-Lie/filtered route.

## Findings

### 1. S_11 mismatch
For
\[
\rho_{11}=[x,y]+z^{[p]}-x^{[p]},
\]
the naive linear model in \(\Lambda^2V\oplus V^{(1)}\) preserves the line through \(z-x\) and gives a stabilizer of order \(|GL_2(p)|\), not the previously stated \(p(p-1)\). Therefore the old derivation cannot be used.

The previously observed \(S_{11}\) orders (6 for p=3, 20 for p=5) require the genuine restricted-power/Jacobson terms. The p=3 corrected calculation already shows that the relation ideal generated by \([x,y]\) cuts the naive stabilizer from 48 to 6.

Classification:
- naive \(\Lambda^2V\oplus V^{(1)}\) derivation of \(S_{11}\): **FAIL / CLOSED**;
- p=3 corrected restricted-Lie calculation: **PASS / LOCAL**;
- general odd-p corrected \(S_{11}\) formula: **OPEN / LOAD-BEARING**.

### 2. Canonical restricted-Lie structure
The correct object must distinguish the canonical ordinary-Lie subspace/image from the Frobenius-twist quotient. In the free restricted Lie algebra, the quotient by the canonical ordinary-Lie part gives the degree-p Frobenius-twist component; a span of chosen symbols \(x^{[p]},y^{[p]}\) is not itself a GL-invariant direct summand.

Thus the previous wording that treated the chosen power span as canonical must be corrected. This potentially removes one obstruction in FRM-0.2, but a canonical splitting/section is not assumed.

Classification:
- canonical quotient interpretation: **PASS / LOCAL**;
- canonical direct-sum section: **OPEN**.

### 3. Free pro-p lifting
For a free pro-p presentation \(F\twoheadrightarrow W_n\), projectivity of free pro-p groups gives a lift of any \(\alpha\in Aut(W_n)\) to an endomorphism of F. Since the lifted map induces an automorphism on the Frattini quotient of F, it is an automorphism of F; kernel equality then gives preservation of the defining normal subgroup.

Hence the earlier blanket rejection of the free-pro-p lift route as a Hopficity issue was too strong. The correct statement is:
- free pro-p lift: **PASS / LOCAL**, subject to the precise presentation and projectivity argument;
- induced action on a chosen relation module: **CONDITIONAL**, because different lifts differ by automorphisms acting trivially on W_n and the quotient action must be shown well-defined modulo that ambiguity.

This does not by itself prove intrinsic finite-window factorization.

### 4. Corrected execution order

**Step 0:** replace the naive relation jet by the genuine restricted-Lie/filtered degree-(2,p) relation object and recompute \(S_{11}\), including Jacobson corrections.

**Step 1:** prove the unconditional graded upper bound
\[
\operatorname{Im}(Aut(W_n)\to GL(V))
\subseteq Stab_{GL(V)}(J),
\]
where \(J\) is the canonical associated-graded relation ideal/shadow. This uses characteristicity of the Zassenhaus filtration and does not require a free-presentation lift.

**Step 2:** isolate the missing coupling information by an intrinsic filtered secondary map (candidate \(\theta\)) connecting the degree-2 relation to its degree-p lift. The key issue is independence under changing lifts by higher filtration terms.

**Step 3:** prove equality by explicit lifting/construction for the stabilizer, using the free-pro-p lift only where its hypotheses are actually satisfied.

**Step 4:** only after a pre-registered formula, use same-prime new-parameter GAP checks (no new prime sweep) and n=p+2 tests as falsification checks.

## Current decision
The single immediate gate remains **P5-JET**, but its formulation is corrected: it is not merely an abstract \(\Lambda^2V\oplus V^{(1)}\) stabilizer calculation. It is a restricted-Lie/filtered relation-jet calculation with Jacobson terms.

No uniform \(p^2\) or \(p^2(p-1)\) theorem is promoted yet.

## Consequence
If Steps 1–3 close, the strongest target is the observed full ratio
\[
p^2(p-1),
\]
not merely its p-primary part \(p^2\). If equality fails, the counterexample becomes a principal negative boundary rather than a reason to adjust the jet ad hoc.
