## Literature signal

Three closely related mechanisms are independently documented in the Demushkin literature.

1. **Serre/Labute Kummer characterization.** For a candidate orientation theta, the maps
   H^1(G,Z_p(theta)/p^n) -> H^1(G,F_p)
   are required to be surjective. For Demushkin groups there is a unique orientation with this property. This is an orientation selector, not merely an existence statement.

2. **Twisted top-degree duality.** For a PD^2 pair and a candidate rho, the orientation is characterized by the size of the top cohomology with the twisted finite module I_m(rho): the orientation is recovered iff
   |H^2(G,I_m(rho))| = p^m
   at every level. By duality this is equivalent to the existence of the identity G-map between the candidate and canonical orientation modules.

3. **One-relator relation obstruction.** For a minimal one-relator pro-p group and a finite coefficient module A_k(rho), the crossed-derivation complex has one top differential A_k^d -> A_k. Thus the twisted top-degree condition can become a finite relation-obstruction condition.

These mechanisms are all conceptually aligned with the project's target fingerprint:

## Standard-family one-relator calculation

For
  G_q = <x1,x2,x3,x4 | r=x1^q[x1,x2][x3,x4]>

and a candidate rho=(u1,u2,u3,u4), rho(r)=1 forces u1^q=1. Since U_k is torsion-free, u1=1.

For a crossed derivation D:G -> A_k(rho), with D(x_j)=1 and D(x_i)=0 for i != j, the relator obstruction is:

  d_rho,1 = q + u2^{-1} - 1
           = (q u2 - u2 + 1)/u2,

  d_rho,2 = 0,

  d_rho,3 = -(u4-1)/(u3 u4),

  d_rho,4 = (u3-1)/(u3 u4).

Thus the top obstruction row vanishes exactly when

  u2 = (1-q)^{-1},   u3=u4=1.

Together with u1=1, this is precisely the standard Demushkin orientation modulo 3^k.

Since the one-relator cochain complex has top term A_k and image generated by this row,

  |H^2(G_q,A_k(rho))| = |A_k| = 3^k

is equivalent, in this standard calculation, to vanishing of the full twisted obstruction row, hence to rho=chi mod 3^k.

This is a major positive discovery: the top-cohomology/Kummer mechanism is not merely existential; on the standard family it produces a **unique finite-level selector**.

## Critical boundary

The calculation above is NOT yet an intrinsic factorization theorem.

Three gates remain load-bearing:

1. **Q_k-factorization.** Although rho and every crossed derivation factor through Q_k, it does not automatically follow that H^2(G,A_k(rho)) is determined by the abstract finite quotient Q_k. Cohomology of G with finite coefficients is not generally a functor of an arbitrary finite quotient.

2. **Presentation/gauge independence.** The explicit row is a one-relator calculation. A genuine carrier must be reformulated as an intrinsic finite extension/obstruction object and shown invariant under change of minimal presentation and relator lift.

3. **Non-tautology.** The PD^2 duality statement itself already knows the canonical orientation. It can serve as the target characterization, but a new filtered carrier must reconstruct the same finite obstruction without simply importing the dualizing module/action.

Therefore the current result is:

- twisted top-cohomology/Kummer selector as a mathematical mechanism: **PASS / LOCAL**;
- unique finite-level selector on the standard one-relator family: **PASS / LOCAL**;
- intrinsic q-blind selector on Q_k: **OPEN**;
- universal factorization through G/P_{k+1}: **OPEN**;
- non-tautological filtered realization: **OPEN**.
