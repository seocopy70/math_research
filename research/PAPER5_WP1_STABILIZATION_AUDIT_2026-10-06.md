For the Zassenhaus filtration D_n(F)=prod_{i p^j >= n} gamma_i(F)^{p^j}, putting n=p+1 does not leave only j=0, i>=p+1.

Indeed: j=0 gives gamma_{p+1}(F); j=1 gives gamma_2(F)^p because 2p >= p+1; j=2 gives F^{p^2}; higher j are absorbed into F^{p^2}. Thus, for odd p,

D_{p+1}(F)=gamma_{p+1}(F) gamma_2(F)^p F^{p^2}

up to the usual closed-product/topological-closure convention.

Therefore D_{p+1}(F)=gamma_{p+1}(F) is false.
