# Paper 5 — W_{p+1}=W_p stabilization claim audit — 2026-10-06

## Classification

**PASS / CLOSED — general odd-p stabilization theorem in the declared presentation.**

The earlier audit incorrectly rejected the stabilization route by (i) truncating the Jennings–Lazard product at gamma_{p+1}, and (ii) treating the relation ideal as if only the [x,y]-line were present. Both objections are superseded.

## 1. Exact Jennings–Lazard product

For D_n(F)=prod_{i p^j >= n} gamma_i(F)^{p^j}, at n=p+1 and odd p:
D_{p+1}(F)=gamma_{p+1}(F) gamma_2(F)^p F^{p^2}
(up to the standard closed-product convention).

Thus D_{p+1}=gamma_{p+1} is FAIL/CLOSED.

## 2. Full degree-two relation layer

Let R=< [x,z], [y,z], [x,y]^{-1} x^p z^{-p} >^F.

Modulo D_3(F), x^p and z^p have weight at least p>=3, so the third relator has initial degree-two form -[x,y]. Together with [x,z] and [y,z], this gives
gr_2(R)=gr_2(F)=span{[x,y],[x,z],[y,z]}.

The earlier audit's assertion that only the [x,y]-line was available was therefore incorrect.

## 3. Degree p+1 propagation

For the Zassenhaus filtration, gr(R) is a restricted Lie ideal of gr(F). Since gr_2(R)=gr_2(F), the ordinary Lie ideal generated by degree two contains the full ordinary free-Lie component in every degree >=2. Since p+1 is not divisible by p, the degree-(p+1) component is the ordinary Lie component; no new p-power generator occurs at that degree. Hence
gr_{p+1}(R)=gr_{p+1}(F).

By the definition of the associated graded, this gives
gamma_{p+1}(F) subset R D_{p+2}(F).

## 4. Exceptional factors

gamma_2(F)^p subset D_{2p}(F) subset D_{p+2}(F), since 2p>=p+2.

F^{p^2} subset D_{p^2}(F) subset D_{p+2}(F), since p^2>=p+2 for p>=3.

Therefore
D_{p+1}(F) subset R D_{p+2}(F).

Consequently
R D_{p+1}=R D_{p+2},
and hence
W_p=F/RD_{p+1} is isomorphic to F/RD_{p+2}=W_{p+1}.

Thus W_{p+1}=W_p is PASS/CLOSED/GENERAL for the declared odd-p presentation.

## 5. Precision boundary

This does NOT prove D_{p+1}(F) subset R. The previously audited counterexample x^{p^2} in D_{p+1}(F) but not in R remains valid.

It also does not by itself close the Paper 5 Step-3 equality Im(Aut(W_n)->GL(V))=S_11(p). That equality remains OPEN/LOAD-BEARING, and the uniform p^2(p-1) automorphism theorem remains CONDITIONAL.
