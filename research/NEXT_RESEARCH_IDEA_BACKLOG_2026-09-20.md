# NEXT_RESEARCH_IDEA_BACKLOG_2026-09-20

## Purpose

This document records research directions that are intentionally **not the immediate execution target**. They remain available for later use and must not silently replace the active main line.

## 1. Finite relation-memory / Fox-residue comparison

Proposal: compare the already constructed intrinsic finite extension carrier
(Q_k, M_k, E_k), its extension class e_k, and transgression coker(delta_rho)
with a presentation-level finite Fox residue of the one-relator 2-cell.

Key question before any construction:
Is the Fox residue genuinely additional information, or is it merely a coordinate realization/functorial image of e_k and the transgression?

If the latter, Fox residue becomes an explanatory coordinate model rather than a new carrier.
If the former, the difference identifies precisely what information the current intrinsic extension carrier omits.

Status: OPEN / deferred. No computation authorized until object, input, gauge, and comparison map are defined.

## 2. Selector theorem as the primary formulation

Reframe the main positive result from "recover chi" to:

"At each finite level k, a finite q-blind twisted obstruction selects a unique candidate orientation character."

Then derive chi mod 3^k as a corollary using PD^2 orientation theory.

Motivation: reduces tautology risk and makes the mathematical mechanism itself the principal theorem.

Status: STRATEGIC CANDIDATE / deferred. It does not replace the need to prove inverse-limit compatibility.

## 3. Maximality-selector formulation

Study whether the finite coker cardinality can be presented as a purely finite maximality principle over candidate rho, avoiding direct reference to H^2 in the carrier definition.

Current known relation:
|coker(delta_rho)| = |H^2(G,A_k(rho))| under the verified finite-level theorem.

Status: OPEN / interpretive branch. Likely a reformulation of the current coker theorem rather than a distinct mechanism.

## 4. Mixed m-adic / weighted relation filtration

Reopen, only at the definition level, the candidate
J_n^mix = I_Fox + m^n, m=(3,I),
or a genuinely bi-filtered variant tracking free degree and 3-adic weight simultaneously.

Potential goal: unify the exponential Zassenhaus versus linear lower-3-central information scales and identify when arithmetic digits become visible.

Hard boundary: ordinary integral augmentation I^2/I^4 is closed; naive degree-3 Fox truncation is closed under Nielsen change.

Status: OPEN / deferred. No numerical scan until a precise intrinsic definition and relator-gauge naturality are proved.

## 5. Information-transfer efficiency as a higher-level theme

Potential synthesis topic:
"How efficiently do intrinsic filtrations transmit arithmetic/orientation information?"

Possible layers:
graded shadow -> filtered extension -> finite 2-cell/transgression -> twisted top obstruction -> orientation.

Potential measurable quantities include first separation depth, coefficient precision, and dependence on filtration choice.

Status: OPEN / long-term synthesis. Current standard-family thresholds are PASS / LOCAL, not yet a universal minimality theorem.

## 6. Universal minimality / admissible-category question

Eventually determine whether P_{k+1} is minimal for finite Kummer recognition, and what "minimal" means after fixing an admissible category of carriers.

Do not claim absolute minimality over arbitrary invariants; that problem is ill-posed without a category.

Status: OPEN / late-stage. Requires the main existence/compatibility theorem first.
