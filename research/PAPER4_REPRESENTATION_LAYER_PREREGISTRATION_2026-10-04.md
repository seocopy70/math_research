# PAPER4_REPRESENTATION_LAYER_PREREGISTRATION_2026-10-04.md

## Reason for reopening

The prior filtered-extension extraction audit correctly ruled out a *new scalar defect attached to a chosen section/lift of the unmarked one-step extension*. It was too strong, however, to interpret that as a no-go for every canonical representation of the same extension or for multi-step tower data.

This audit therefore reopens only a bounded **representation-layer feasibility gate**. It is not a return to ad hoc carrier hunting.

## Fixed object

For the critical index n=p^s, fix the intrinsic filtered tower segment
\[
W_{n+2}\to W_{n+1}\to W_n
\]
and, when needed, the full finite Zassenhaus tower through a predeclared finite depth.

No new invariant is introduced by name.

## Pre-registered structure layers

The only admissible representations of the fixed object are:

1. the central extension class of
\[
1\to D_n/D_{n+1}\to W_{n+1}\to W_n\to1;
\]
2. the induced conjugation/module structure;
3. canonical restricted p-power and commutator operations on graded layers;
4. the compatible interaction of these operations across adjacent layers;
5. the two-step/full finite tower extension data, including higher extension compatibility.

A proposed detector must be a functorial construction from these predeclared structures. No new carrier, character, or ad hoc lift decoration may be introduced merely because an earlier layer fails.

## E_psi mechanism decomposition

The marked affine detector has two logically distinct ingredients:

- **filtered depth:** the target E_s has D_{p^s}(E_s) nontrivial and D_{p^s+1}(E_s)=1, producing the critical finite-window threshold;
- **orientation/representation:** the chosen character psi and crossed cocycle delta supply a directed affine coordinate in which the hidden relation is evaluated.

Thus the success of E_psi is not attributable to filtered depth alone. It is a combination of filtered depth and an oriented representation.

The intrinsic question is therefore not “can the unmarked one-step extension itself select psi?” (already FAIL/CLOSED), but:

> can the predeclared tower structures canonically determine an orbit/groupoid of admissible affine representations, or an equivalent representation-theoretic object, without selecting a distinguished psi?

This is a bounded structural question, not arbitrary candidate hunting.

## Representability formulation

Do not use “representable” as a slogan. First define a category C_n whose objects are admissible filtered extension/tower structures at the fixed depth and whose morphisms are filtered isomorphisms. Define a set-valued or groupoid-valued functor
\[
F_{\mathrm{aff}}
\]
sending a tower object to its admissible affine representations/crossed-derivation data satisfying the critical-window conditions.

The first legitimate test is whether F_aff is determined functorially by the predeclared tower structure and whether its orbit/groupoid is invariant under C_n-isomorphism.

A genuine representing object is optional; it is not assumed to exist.

## Failure taxonomy

Every negative result must be classified as one of:

1. **Representation failure:** the chosen coordinate/presentation cannot express information that is present in the fixed structure.
2. **Information failure:** even the full predeclared structure is identical/isomorphic while the target defect differs.
3. **Detector failure:** the structure contains the relevant information, but no functorial extraction operator has yet been identified.

Only (2) supports a structural no-go for the declared object. (1) and (3) do not.

## Multi-step boundary

Failure at the one-step extension is not promoted to tower no-go. The minimum next object is the two-step tower
\[
W_{p^s+2}\to W_{p^s+1}\to W_{p^s}.
\]
If its compatible extension operations still do not determine an affine representation groupoid, classify the result accordingly. No arbitrary tower-wide search is authorized.

## Predictions

P1: the one-step extension class alone does not canonically select a single psi. Already **FAIL/CLOSED** by symmetry.

P2: the filtered extension plus canonical restricted operations may determine an affine *orbit/groupoid* even when it cannot determine a single psi. **OPEN / LOAD-BEARING**.

P3: if the two-step tower still carries no functorial affine groupoid/defect, this is a stronger information-failure candidate; but it must be distinguished from detector failure before any no-go is claimed.

## Stop rule

After testing the five predeclared layers and the two-step boundary, stop this representation-layer branch. Do not invent additional carriers. The outcome must be PASS / CLOSED, CONDITIONAL, FAIL / CLOSED, or OPEN with a precise failure mode.
