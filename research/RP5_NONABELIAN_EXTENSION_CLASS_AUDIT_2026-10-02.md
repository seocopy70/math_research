## Decision

The corrected RP-5 branch reaches a genuine **graph-sensitive separation result** on a minimal four-vertex specially oriented subclass, but does **not** yet prove full directed-incidence reconstruction for arbitrary specially oriented graphs.

The decisive object is the filtered central extension
[

A separate convention correction is load-bearing: in Blumer–Quadrelli–Weigel, a special edge is ((v,w)) with (v) ordinary and (w) special, and

## 8. What this does NOT prove

It does not prove that the entire directed incidence matrix of an arbitrary specially oriented graph is recoverable.

In general:

- ordinary degree-2 commutators can contaminate the attempt to identify the special sector as a centralizer;
- multiple special edges can share origins or termini;
- the raw degree-q extension defect can mix with lower-degree structure;
- a canonical splitting (L_1=Ooplus S) is not generally available.

Therefore criterion C remains open.

The correct current statement is:

[
oxed{
	ext{Criterion B: PASS / LOCAL}
}
]

not

[
	ext{Criterion C: PASS}.
