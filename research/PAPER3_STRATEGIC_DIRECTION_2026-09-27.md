# PAPER 3 STRATEGIC DIRECTION — 2026-09-27

## Purpose

This document records the strategic interpretation of the 2026-09-27 prior-art audit and the decision not to abandon the original long-term generalization goal.

## 1. Ultimate goal remains active

The original motivation for Paper 3 remains valid:

> move from the completed, relatively specialized Demuškin/Kummer results toward a general theory of how finite filtered information determines global invariants.

The prior-art audit does not force the project to remain inside a narrow Demuškin application. Instead, it changes the level of abstraction at which the generalization should be attempted.

The intended generalization is no longer merely:
- rank/general-group generalization;
- another application of the Paper 2 affine theorem;
- another specific cohomological target.

The preferred axis is a general theory of finite-window recognition for filtered algebraic objects.

## 2. What prior art closes

The following broad slogans are not novelty claims:
- finite information can determine a global invariant;
- finite quotients can determine cohomological information;
- minimal determining quotients exist in important classes;
- Zassenhaus-level information can determine cohomological/representation-theoretic data.

Efrat–Mináč and related finite-determinacy literature are direct prior art and must be treated as background.

## 3. What remains potentially new

The current candidate structure is the category-relative threshold

r_T(C;D_•) = min { n : W_n(G) ≅ W_n(H) => T(G) ≅ T(H) for all G,H in C }

together with a separate factorization threshold.

Potentially nontrivial axes are:
1. factorization threshold versus recognition threshold;
2. explicit separation where the two thresholds differ;
3. filtration dependence of recognition thresholds;
4. category dependence and comparison theorems;
5. sharp same-window/different-target lower bounds;
6. structural conditions for finite recognizability.

None of these is currently promoted to a novelty claim. Each requires theorem-level verification and further prior-art comparison.

## 4. Strategic meaning

The long-term program therefore remains:

Paper 1 → finite recognition of a concrete invariant

Paper 2 → sharp finite factorization/information boundary

New Paper 3 → make finite-window recognition itself a mathematical object and begin its general theory

Longer-term target → a general finite-window recognition theory for filtered algebraic objects.

Thus the current Paper 3 is not a retreat to a narrower topic. It is an attempt to generalize the question rather than merely generalize the class of groups.

## 5. Immediate research consequence

Before broad computation, the next decisive task is to test whether factorization and recognition are genuinely independent.

Primary search:

n_fac(T) != r_T

via an explicit separation pair.

If no genuine separation is found in natural categories, descend to a controlled abstract T_beta model to determine whether the distinction is logically substantive or collapses under natural hypotheses.
