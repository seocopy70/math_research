# ORIENTATION MOD-9 — E1–E5 CLOSURE / HAND AUDIT — 2026-09-19

## Scope

This is a no-scan hand audit of the projective degree-(2,3) relation-jet route after the residual-gauge lemma.

The target is **recovery of the orientation character modulo 9**, not the full \\(\mathbb Z_3^\\times\\)-valued character.

## 1. Exact carrier

For a minimal free pro-3 presentation
\\[
1\to R\to F\to G\to1,
\\]
write the filtered defining relator to degree 3 as
\\[
r=(R_2,P_3)+O(4),
\\]
where
\\[
R_2\in L_2(F),\qquad P_3\in L_3^{res}(F).
\\]

The relevant datum is not a normalized pair but its projective relation jet
\\[
J_3=\langle(R_2,P_3)\rangle.
\\]

The recovery functional is
\\[
\Theta_J(\lambda)(f)
=f(P_3)+(\lambda\wedge f)(R_2),
\\qquad f,\lambda\in V^*=H^1(G,\mathbf F_3).
\\]

For the frozen q=3 presentation,
\\[
R_2=e_1\wedge e_2+e_3\wedge e_4,
\qquad
P_3=e_1^{[3]}.
\\]

Hence
\\[
\Theta_J(\lambda)(f)
=(1-a_2)f_1+a_1f_2-a_4f_3+a_3f_4
\\]
for \\(\lambda=\sum a_i e_i^*\\).

## 2. Presentation-change audit

Let \\(\alpha:F\to F\\) induce id_G.

Minimality gives no degree-one change:
\\[
\alpha(X_i)=X_i+Q_i+O(3).
\\]

Because \\(\alpha(x_i)x_i^{-1}\in R\\) and the initial ideal is generated by the single quadratic line \\(\langle R_2\rangle\\),
\\[
Q_i=c_iR_2.
\\]

Therefore
\\[
\alpha(R_2)=R_2+[R_2,v]\pmod{L_{\ge4}},
\\]
where
\\[
v=c_1X_2-c_2X_1+c_3X_4-c_4X_3.
\\]

A change of relation-module generator can additionally multiply the relation class by a unit \\(u\in\mathbf Z_3^\\times\\). Thus, at the degree-(2,3) level relevant here,
\\[
(R_2,P_3)\longmapsto
(uR_2,uP_3+[v,R_2]).
\\]

For every \\(f\in V^*\\),
\\[
f([v,R_2])=0,
\\]
because a degree-one functional annihilates brackets. Consequently
\\[
\Theta'(\lambda)=u\,\Theta(\lambda).
\\]

Therefore the zero set of \\(\Theta\\) is independent of the chosen minimal free cover, free lift, relator conjugation, and common relation-module normalization.

This is the precise invariant needed for recovery. It is stronger and safer to claim invariance of the **Theta zero set** than literal equality of a normalized jet.

## 3. Why different minimal free presentations are covered

If two minimal free presentations of the same finitely generated pro-3 group are given, lifts between them exist by freeness. The induced maps on abelianizations are isomorphisms because both presentations are minimal and both abelianizations identify with \\(G_{ab}\\). Hence the lifted maps are isomorphisms by the pro-p Burnside basis criterion. Their comparison reduces to the identity-on-G case above.

Thus the degree-(2,3) ambiguity already audited is the relevant presentation-change ambiguity.

## 4. Automorphism naturality

For an automorphism \\(\varphi\in\operatorname{Aut}(G)\\), lift it to a free presentation. Its action on \\(V\\) is the induced linear map \\(g\\). The relation jet transforms by the same projective scaling plus bracket gauge described above.

The expression
\\[
f(P_3)+(\lambda\wedge f)(R_2)
\\]
is functorial under the induced linear change of \\(V\\), while the extra bracket gauge vanishes after evaluation by \\(f\\). Hence the zero set is transported naturally.

Therefore the construction does not depend on frozen coordinates or on the preferred free generators.

## 5. Separation q=3 versus q=infinity

For q=3:
\\[
(R_2,P_3)=
([X_1,X_2]+[X_3,X_4],X_1^{[3]}).
\\]

For q=infinity:
\\[
(R_2,P_3)=
([X_1,X_2]+[X_3,X_4],0).
\\]

The quadratic form \\(R_2\\) is the same, but the degree-3 relation component differs.

Because \\(R_2\\) is nondegenerate alternating, the map
\\[
\Phi_R(\lambda)(f)=(\lambda\wedge f)(R)
\\]
is an isomorphism. Thus:

- q=infinity has unique zero \\(\lambda=0\\);
- q=3 has unique zero \\(\lambda=e_2^*\\).

So the carrier genuinely separates the two cases without inserting q into the definition.

## 6. Identification with the orientation character modulo 9

For the q=3 presentation,
\\[
\lambda_\chi=e_2^*.
\\]

Hence
\\[
\rho_\chi(x_i)=1+3\lambda_\chi(x_i)\pmod9
=(1,4,1,1).
\\]

This agrees with the canonical Demuškin orientation value
\\[
\chi(x_2)=(1-3)^{-1}\equiv4\pmod9,
\\]
and the other three generators have value 1 modulo 9 in the frozen presentation.

Thus the unique intrinsic zero recovers
\\[
\boxed{\chi\pmod9}.
\\]

## 8. What is NOT proved

1. The projective jet \\(J_3\\) is mathematically minimal in an absolute categorical sense.
2. The bare associated graded restricted Lie object recovers \\(\chi\\); in fact the q=3/q=infinity comparison proves the opposite.
3. The complete \\(\mathbf Z_3^\\times\\)-valued character \\(\chi\\) has been reconstructed. The present result is only the first mod-9 digit.
4. A canonical higher-​3-adic tower recovering all digits has not been constructed.
