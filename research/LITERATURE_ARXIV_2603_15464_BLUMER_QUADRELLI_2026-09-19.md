# Literature Audit — arXiv:2603.15464v2

## Bibliographic identity

Simone Blumer and Claudio Quadrelli, *Variations of Demuškin groups that are not absolute Galois groups and their Lie algebras*, arXiv:2603.15464v2.

## Full-paper relevance audit

### Canonical orientation

For a Demuškin group the paper records the canonical orientation
[
\theta_G(y_1)=(1-q)^{-1}.
]
At q=3 this gives
[
\theta_G(y_1)=-1/2.
]
This independently matches the project's exact q=3 crossed-derivation recovery
[
\rho(x_2)=-1/2.
]

### Information loss at the quadratic graded level

For the family
[
\mathcal F_1=
\langle x_1,y_1,\ldots,x_d,y_d
\mid [x_1^q,y_1]\cdots[x_d,y_d]=1\rangle_{\hat p},
]
the q-power contribution disappears from the initial quadratic relation. The associated graded group-algebra relation is of the form
[
[X_2,Y_2]+\cdots+[X_d,Y_d].
]
Thus q-sensitive information present in the original relation/orientation is not necessarily retained by the quadratic graded shadow.

This is an independent literature example supporting the project's closed negative boundary
[
\operatorname{gr}_{\mathrm{quad}}\not\Rightarrow\chi.
]

### Open-subgroup q-information

The paper computes, for a suitable index-p subgroup U,
[
U^{ab}\cong \mathbf Z_p^{2(d-1)p+1}\times(\mathbf Z/q)^p.
]
This shows that q can reappear in richer subgroup-level structure even when absent from the original low-degree associated graded relation.

### Relation to the current carrier

The paper studies cyclotomicity/orientation and Lie-algebraic shadows, but it does not construct the project's projective degree-(2,3) relation carrier, does not prove its gauge invariance, and does not reconstruct the full character
[
\chi:G\to\mathbf Z_3^\times
]
from that carrier.

Therefore this paper is supporting literature, not a proof of the current reconstruction theorem.
