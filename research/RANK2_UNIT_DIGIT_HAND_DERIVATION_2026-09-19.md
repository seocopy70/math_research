## 1. Relation module and relator unit

Let F be the free pro-3 group on x1,x2, R the closed normal subgroup generated by
r_q=x1^q[x1,x2].

For the one-relator presentation, the relation module
M=R/[R,F]
is a free rank-one Z_3-module once the relator class [r_q] is chosen as generator.

For an actual phi in Aut(G_q), any free lift \tilde phi preserves R and induces
\tilde phi([r_q])=u(phi)[r_q], u(phi) in Z_3^times.

If two free lifts differ by an automorphism acting trivially modulo R, their difference on R is generated by commutators with elements of R; hence they induce the same action on R/[R,F]. Thus u(phi) is independent of the chosen free lift, subject to the stated relation-module convention.

## 2. Multiplicativity

Because the induced action on M is scalar,

u(phi psi)[r_q]
 = phi_*(psi_*[r_q])
 = u(phi)u(psi)[r_q].

Therefore

u(phi psi)=u(phi)u(psi).

So, after fixing the generator/sign convention of M, u is a genuine character Aut(G_q)->Z_3^times, not merely a crossed homomorphism.

This also means the first 3-adic digit of u is a multiplicative character. The additive digit on 1+3Z_3 is obtained only after proving u mod 3=1 on the relevant subgroup.

## 3. q=infinity control

For q=infinity,

G_infinity^(2)=<x1,x2 | [x1,x2]>=Z_3^2.

An integral automorphism A in GL_2(Z_3) sends the basic commutator to

[x1,x2]^{det A}

in the relation module (with the chosen commutator convention).

Hence

u_infinity(phi)=det(A)

for the natural integral presentation convention.

Therefore u mod 9 is already visible from the integral linear action in the abelian case. This is a control, not yet a filtered-observability theorem.

## 4. q=3: orientation constraint derived, but u mod 9 is NOT yet derived

For

r_3=x1^3[x1,x2],

the canonical Demushkin orientation, derived by requiring arbitrary chi-crossed derivations to kill r_3, has

chi(x1)=1,
chi(x2)=(1-3)^(-1)=(-2)^(-1).

This agrees with the standard odd-prime Demushkin orientation pattern; literature confirms the existence/functoriality of the canonical orientation, but the exact equality u=chi for the relator module remains unproved here.

Since every automorphism preserves the canonical orientation, write the abelianized action as

phi(x1)_ab = x1^a x2^(3c),
phi(x2)_ab = x1^b x2^d.

Applying chi gives

(-2)^(-3c)=1,

so c=0, and

(-2)^(-d)=(-2)^(-1),

so d=1.

Thus the abelianized automorphism has the form

[[a,b],[0,1]]

with a in (Z/3Z)^times.

The degree-2 cup-product preservation then forces the mod-3 determinant to be 1, hence a=1 mod 3. Consequently the induced mod-3 matrix of an actual automorphism is of the form

g = [[1,b],[0,1]]

rather than an arbitrary GL_2(F_3) matrix.

In particular the tested q=3 exclusion of -I is structurally consistent with this constraint.
