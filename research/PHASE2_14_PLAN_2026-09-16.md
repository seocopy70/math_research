## Why an extension field is needed

\(\mathbf F_3^\times\) has order 2, so the Phase 2-13C character sees only sign/parity information. It cannot by itself recover a full algebraic C2 highest weight.

The next probe should therefore work over \(\mathbf F_9\), using an exact finite-field implementation, not floating-point arithmetic.

## Phase 2-14A: exact F9 construction

1. Construct \(\mathbf F_9=\mathbf F_3[\omega]/(\omega^2+1)\).
2. Implement exact addition, multiplication, inversion and equality for field elements represented as pairs \((a,b)\), \(a+b\omega\).
3. Lift the standard C2 symplectic form and the 4-dimensional natural module from \(\mathbf F_3\) to \(\mathbf F_9\).
4. Construct split-torus elements
\[
 t(a,b)=\operatorname{diag}(a,b,a^{-1},b^{-1}),
\qquad a,b\in\mathbf F_9^\times.
\]
5. Verify symplecticity and exact torus multiplication laws.

## Phase 2-14B: weight of the fixed line

The existing \(35\)-dimensional quotient representation must be genuinely extended to \(\mathbf F_9\). It is not sufficient to reinterpret the old F3 matrices as arbitrary F9 torus actions.

Construct the action of the F9 torus on the underlying degree-4 Lie representation, pass through the relation quotient and the already-certified \(W/U\) construction, and obtain the action on
\[
E_{\mathbf F_9}=E\otimes_{\mathbf F_3}\mathbf F_9.
\]

Verify that the unique F3 fixed line extends to a one-dimensional F9 fixed line and determine its torus character on enough elements of \(T(\mathbf F_9)\) to distinguish candidate weights.

## Phase 2-14D: generated-submodule test

Starting from the unique \(U^+\)-fixed vector, generate the H/subgroup submodule under the appropriate negative-root or full Chevalley generators and compute its dimension and composition structure.

Compare this with the candidate highest-weight module. In particular, record whether the generated submodule is all of E.

## Phase 2-14E: final intertwiner certificate

Only if the candidate survives the previous tests, construct an explicit intertwiner between E and the candidate module and verify every generator equation exactly over the relevant field.

The final conclusion must be one of:

- candidate disproved;
- candidate supported but not yet identified;
- full module isomorphism certified.
