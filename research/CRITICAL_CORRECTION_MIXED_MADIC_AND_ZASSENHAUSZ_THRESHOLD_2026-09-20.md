# CRITICAL CORRECTION — MIXED m-ADIC GATE + ZASSENHAUSZ THRESHOLD — 2026-09-20

## Purpose

This audit records a correction to the previous review. The proposed Zassenhaus threshold argument did **not** assert
\[
G/D_N(G)\cong G^{ab}/3^{s+1}.
\]
The intended statement concerns the **abelianization of the finite quotient**:
\[
(G/D_N(G))^{ab}=G/(D_N(G)G').
\]
The earlier wording was ambiguous and is corrected here.

## 1. Abelianization calculation

For the standard family
\[
G_q=\langle x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4\mid x_1^q[x_1,x_2][x_3,x_4]=1\rangle,
\qquad q=3^s,
\]
the commutator factor disappears in abelianization, so
\[
G_q^{ab}\cong \mathbf Z/3^s\times\mathbf Z_3^3.
\]

For a free pro-3 group F, the Zassenhaus/Jennings formula is
\[
D_N(F)=\prod_{i3^j\ge N}\gamma_i(F)^{3^j}.
\]
After passing to abelianization, all factors with i>=2 vanish. Hence the image of D_N(F) in F^{ab} is the subgroup generated by 3^jF^{ab} with 3^j>=N.

For
\[
N=3^s+1,
\]
the least such j is s+1. Therefore
\[
(G_{3^s}/D_N(G_{3^s}))^{ab}
\cong
\mathbf Z/3^s\times(\mathbf Z/3^{s+1})^3.
\]
For the power-free control group G_infty,
\[
(G_\infty/D_N(G_\infty))^{ab}
\cong
(\mathbf Z/3^{s+1})^4.
\]

Thus the finite quotients are already separated at the level of their abelianizations. No claim that the whole quotient is abelian is being made.

## 2. Why N<=3^s cannot see the power relation

Let F be free and let c=[x_1,x_2][x_3,x_4]. For N<=3^s,
\[
x_1^{3^s}\in D_{3^s}(F)\subseteq D_N(F).
\]
Hence, modulo D_N(F),
\[
x_1^{3^s}c\equiv c.
\]
Consequently
\[
F/\langle D_N(F),x_1^{3^s}c\rangle
\cong
F/\langle D_N(F),c\rangle.
\]
By functoriality of the Zassenhaus filtration under epimorphisms, this gives
\[
G_{3^s}/D_N(G_{3^s})
\cong
G_\infty/D_N(G_\infty)
\qquad(N\le3^s).
\]

This is the precise indistinguishability statement. It is stronger and cleaner than an abelianization argument.

## 3. Consequence for chi mod 3^n

For the standard Demushkin family,
\[
\chi_{3^s}(x_2)=(1-3^s)^{-1},
\qquad
\chi_\infty(x_2)=1.
\]
Modulo 3^n, the valuation of \(\chi_{3^s}(x_2)-1\) is s. Hence the residue is determined by whether s<n and, when s<n, by the corresponding valuation layer.

Using the previous two sections:

- \(N=3^{n-1}+1\) is sufficient to distinguish all cases relevant to \(\chi\bmod3^n\) within the standard family;
- \(N=3^{n-1}\) is insufficient, because G_{3^{n-1}} and G_infty have the same quotient at that level while their orientations differ modulo 3^n.

For n=2 this gives the threshold D_4; for n=3 it gives D_10.

This is an information-boundary statement **for the standard Demushkin family and its quotient-isomorphism information**, not a universal pointed naturality theorem and not an orientation-free carrier theorem.

## 6. Gate correction

The project must distinguish:

### Existence / admissibility gate
A finite intrinsic carrier is admissible even if it is obtained from a finite Fox-derived construction, provided intrinsicity and the orientation bridge are proved.

### Non-tautology / compression gate
Only after existence is established do we ask whether the carrier is genuinely smaller, independently characterized, or merely Fox under another name.

Therefore failure of a chosen Fox truncation must not be promoted to a no-go for all mixed finite carriers.
