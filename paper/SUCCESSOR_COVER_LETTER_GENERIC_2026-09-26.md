# Generic submission cover letter — successor manuscript

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript

**Sharp Finite-Window Recognition and Information Loss in Oriented Pro-(p) Groups**

for publication.

The paper studies the exact Zassenhaus depth needed to preserve all continuous affine crossed-cocycle representations at a fixed coefficient level. For the standard Demushkin family of odd-prime type, the main result identifies the sharp depth
[
n_{\mathrm{aff}}(k)=p^{k-1}+1
]
for the category of affine representations into
[
A_k\rtimes U_{1,k},
qquad
A_k=\mathbb Z/p^k\mathbb Z.
]
The lower bound is explicit, includes the rank-two boundary, and does not require surjectivity of the orientation component.

The paper also separates three logically different issues: representation-theoretic factorization, recognition of the canonical orientation, and absolute information sufficiency. In particular, it records an information-loss boundary showing why a finite-window factorization theorem should not by itself be interpreted as a universal orientation-recognition theorem.

The manuscript is deliberately restricted in scope. It does not claim discovery of the classical Demushkin orientation, absolute minimality among arbitrary intrinsic carriers, or a universal theorem for all elementary-type pro-(p) constructions. The novelty statement is correspondingly formulated as a conditional literature boundary rather than an absolute priority claim.

The manuscript has undergone an independent referee-style mathematical and prior-art audit, and the publication candidate has been subjected to automated LaTeX/PDF verification. The final submission branch is being frozen only after the corresponding CI verification is green.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
